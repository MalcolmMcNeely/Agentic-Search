# Dream Store

> **Covers:** the Dream Realm shop: what currency it takes, what it stocks, the monthly limit, and the per-tier Soul Sigil costs a purchase pays into.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-30

A shop inside the **Emporium**, paid for with **Dream Fragments**. It sells copies of heroes, so it is a route to ascension that does not go through a banner.

## Currency

**Dream Fragments come from Dream Realm and nowhere else that matters.** Two payouts, both daily:

- Damage dealt to the day's boss, banded by the percentage of its HP removed.
- Daily rank against the server.

So the store's budget is set by Dream Realm performance. Push the boss harder and the shelf gets cheaper. See [snow-stomper.md](snow-stomper.md) and [skyclops.md](skyclops.md) for the two bosses written up, and [game-modes.md](game-modes.md) for the mode.

`unverified`: one source summary adds the Season Store and hero-locked Temporal Shards as fragment sources. Every other source names Dream Realm alone. The account is permanent-track only, so a Season Store route pays it nothing either way. See [../account/progression.md](../account/progression.md).

## What it stocks

`disputed`, and the account wins.

Every guide says the shelf holds **Soul Sigils for every A-Level hero**, 15 copies each per month. That claim traces back to a single gameleap article dated **11 April 2024**, launch-era, and every later source repeats it. The account contradicts it: **Daimon and Bonnie are both absent from the live shelf**, and Bonnie is A-Level on every reading. So the shelf is a subset, not the whole A-Level pool, and no source names which subset.

**The shelf has to be read in game.** Until the live list is recorded in [../account/progression.md](../account/progression.md), no purchase can be recommended from this file.

The store also carries **hero-locked Temporal Essence**, which converts to universal Temporal Essence once that hero is already invested in. Its stock rotates daily.

**No S-Level hero is ever stocked.** Thoran, Shakir and every other S-Level pick stay on their banners. See [recruitment.md](recruitment.md). Celestial and Hypogean heroes have their own shelf in the Guild Store, paid in Guild Medals. See [guild-store.md](guild-store.md).

**Rare heroes cannot ascend at all**, so Hammie and Chippy are irrelevant here. See [progression-systems.md](progression-systems.md).

## Limit and price

- **15 copies per hero, per month.** Stock resets monthly. Same 2024 source, so treat it as `unverified` alongside the shelf itself.
- Price is `unverified`. gameleap reads **1,200 Dream Fragments per copy**. The same 1,200 figure is reported elsewhere as the total for one Soul Sigil, which is a different claim. Check the price tag in game before budgeting a month around it.

## What a copy buys

Ascending an A-Level hero costs Soul Sigils per step, not cumulatively:

| Step | Soul Sigils |
| --- | --- |
| Elite -> Elite+ | 1 |
| Elite+ -> Epic | 3 |
| Epic -> Epic+ | 4 |
| Epic+ -> Legendary | 5 |
| Legendary -> Legendary+ | 8 |
| Legendary+ -> Mythic | 10 |
| Mythic -> **Mythic+** | **12** |
| Mythic+ -> Supreme | 200 Faction Acorns, no sigils |
| Supreme -> Supreme+ | 20 |

The sigil column sums to 63, which matches both sources' "1 original plus 63 duplicates, 64 in total". Two independent sources agree on the per-step numbers, so this table is the solid part of the file.

## How to pick, once the shelf is known

Rank by what the next tier actually unlocks, not by tier list. Only three tiers change anything, per [progression-systems.md](progression-systems.md):

- **Legendary+** gives Hero Focus, a stat buff or passive.
- **Mythic+** gives Exclusive Equipment, the only tier that adds a skill.
- **Supreme+** gives Enhance Force, which upgrades one existing skill.

So the buy order is: a hero one step under **Mythic+** first, then a hero one step under **Legendary+**, then depth. A hero already past Mythic+ and short of Supreme+ is buying stats only.

Weigh that against how many fielded comps the hero stands in, from [../account/teams.md](../account/teams.md). A hero in four comps returns the sigils four times over.

**The account owns no Exclusive Equipment on any hero.** Any stocked hero sitting at Mythic is therefore worth 12 sigils on its own, because it delivers the account's first EX weapon and a new skill. See [../account/roster.md](../account/roster.md).

**Kruger is a weak buy despite sitting at Legendary+.** His Hero Focus grants Ranged DEF and no damage at all, per [heroes/kruger.md](heroes/kruger.md), and he appears in one comp.

**The budget is the real constraint, not the shelf.** The account sits at Snow Stomper, Epic difficulty in Dream Realm. Fragment income scales with boss damage, so raising Dream Realm output is what makes this shop affordable. See [snow-stomper.md](snow-stomper.md).
