# Darkswarm Invasion

> **Covers:** the Homestead invasion event, what triggers it, how it is scored, and what a team needs.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-30

## What it is

A **Homestead** event, not a battle mode in the Everlight. Hypofiends attack the Homestead, damage it, and loot it for **24 hours**. They cut harvester efficiency while they sit there. Beat them and the invasion ends early, and the defeated Hypofiends give back everything they looted.

## What triggers it

**Aggression.** Building, upgrading and using Homestead buildings raises the Aggression level. Cross the threshold and an invasion fires. Both the threshold and the Aggression each upgrade adds scale with Homestead level, so a bigger Homestead is invaded on its own schedule.

Aggression is not a punishment. It is the cost of building, and the invasion is the mode's content.

## How it is scored

**Weekly contribution is the total damage dealt across the week.** Contribution and its rewards reset **Monday 19:00 UTC-5**.

That makes it a **damage race, not a kill check**. A battle that ends in a wipe still banks every point of damage it dealt. Defending another player's Homestead pays contribution the same way.

Attempts are **limited per Hypofiend battle**, and no source publishes the number. Treat every attempt as spent, not retried.

## Assist

Other players request help when their Homestead is invaded. Answering one opens a **30-day mutual assist period** between the two accounts. Guildmates and friends can be invited to defend, which is how a battle above the account's own strength gets cleared.

## What a team needs

1. **Damage over the whole fight, not burst.** The score is cumulative damage, so a comp that survives and snowballs banks more than a comp that spikes and dies.
2. **AoE.** The enemy is a swarm. `unverified`: no source states whether one battle fields one Hypofiend or a wave, so wave clear is the safer build.
3. **Both damage types, if the comp can afford it.** The Hypofiend faction and resistances are not published, so a single-damage-type comp is a gamble.

**No faction counter is plannable.** No source names the Hypofiends' faction. Build for raw output and the faction stat bonus, not for the +15% counter. See [factions.md](factions.md).

**Battles run on auto-play.** `unverified`, one source. The comp and its stats decide the result, not input.

## Not recorded

- Attempts per Hypofiend battle.
- Hypofiend faction, damage type and resistances.
- Whether a battle is one enemy or a wave.
- The Homestead level and Aggression level on this account. See [../account/progression.md](../account/progression.md).
