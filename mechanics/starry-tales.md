# Starry Tales

> **Covers:** what Starry Tales is, how it is paid out, what its rewards are worth to this account, and what is still unknown about it.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-31

**Starry Tales is a permanent story feature, not an event and not a battle mode.** It holds one exclusive side story per hero, telling that hero's own tale in Esperia. It arrived on **5 March 2026** and stories are still being added patch by patch.

It does not appear in [game-modes.md](game-modes.md) because it is not a mode. Nothing in it is timed, so nothing in it expires.

## How it fills up

New stories ship in patches, one or two at a time:

| Story | Hero | Added in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Final Verdict | Athalia | before 1.7.2 |
| Firepower Fanatic | Gunnar | 1.7.2, 2 July 2026 |
| Song of the Blizzard | Alna | 1.7.3, 6 August 2026 |
| Dreamy Dawn | Aurora | not recorded |

`unverified`: **the total number of stories live now.** The Fandom list page returns a count of 1 in one search summary and 2 in another, while the patch notes and story pages together name the four above. The wiki index is behind its own content. Read the count off the Starry Tales screen in game.

## Rewards

**Confirmed for one story only: Final Verdict pays 200 Diamonds and 2 Temporal Essence.**

`unverified`: whether every story pays the same. Only Final Verdict has a published reward line. Treat 200 Diamonds and 2 Temporal Essence as the shape of the payout, not as a guaranteed rate.

## Is it worth doing

**Yes, and the Temporal Essence is the reason, not the diamonds.**

**Temporal Essence cannot be bought.** It is farmed from Dream Realm top 100, any Seasonal Dream Realm rank, Arena rank-ups, the Guild Chest and Legend Trial clears. Every other route is a performance gate. Starry Tales is not, so it is free essence for reading text.

**This account is standing in the exact band that spends it.** Taichi & Agumon is **Supreme** at **EX +8**, and EX levels **6 to 10** are bought with Temporal Essence. See [../account/equipment.md](../account/equipment.md) and [equipment-and-charms.md](equipment-and-charms.md).

**The diamonds are small but real.** 200 a story against a 17,000 stock is under a day's income, and the spend order in [diamonds.md](diamonds.md) already has a standing 2,400-a-month order on Stellar Crystals. Take them, do not plan around them.

**It costs nothing but time.** No stamina, no attempt limit, no team, no roster requirement that any source names. There is no case for skipping it.

## Open questions

Three things no source covers. All three are one glance at the game screen.

- **Where Starry Tales sits in the UI.** No guide names the screen it lives on.
- **Whether a story needs its hero owned.** Athalia, Gunnar and Alna are not on this roster; Aurora is. If ownership gates a story, only Dreamy Dawn is currently readable. See [../account/roster.md](../account/roster.md).
- **Whether stories carry battles.** Every source calls them stories, none mentions a fight, and none rules one out.
