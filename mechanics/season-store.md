# Season Store

> **Covers:** the Season Token shelf in the Emporium, what it sells, why it is the route by which a season pays out in permanent goods, and what to buy on this account.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-10

The third of the repo's shop files, alongside [guild-store.md](guild-store.md) and [dream-store.md](dream-store.md).

## It is not season-only, and that is the point

**The Season Store sells permanent goods for Season Tokens.** Its listed availability reads "September 19, 2024 – Indefinite", and **Season Tokens are named in no reset list**, so tokens earned in one season are spendable in the next.

`unverified`: whether Season Tokens truly survive a reset. The Season page's reset list does not name them, which is evidence but not a statement. The only source that says outright that they do not reset is dated 2024-11-28, outside the 12-month window, and is not carried here. **Read the token balance on the morning of 2026-09-24 and the question is settled.**

**The shelf resets monthly**, on the calendar month, not on the season.

## What it stocks

- **Soul Sigils of heroes released during the season.** This is the headline item: a sigil is a permanent ascension step, and Soul Sigils are the account's tightest constraint. See [../account/progression.md](../account/progression.md).
- **Abyssal Essence.**
- **EX Equipment Reset (+15).**
- **Dream Fragments**, which are the Dream Store's own currency. See [dream-store.md](dream-store.md).
- **Arena Coins.**

## What this means for this account

**Season Tokens are the one seasonal currency that buys permanent progress, so they are the season currency to prioritise.** Every other season currency ends at the reset with nothing to show for it beyond the settlement.

**The sigil shelf is the strongest item on it.** The account has three cheap Celestial and Hypogean ascensions queued at 2 and 3 sigils each, and its whole Arena plan turns on ascension because Arena caps everything else. See [../mechanics/arena.md](../mechanics/arena.md).

**The catch is the wording: sigils of heroes released *during the season*.** For Crown of Ashes that means the Season 7 hero releases, not the account's existing queue. Read the live shelf before planning a purchase.
