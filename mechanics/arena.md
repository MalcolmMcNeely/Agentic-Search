# Arena

> **Covers:** the Arena rank ladder, the maps and who chooses them, the daily attempt limits, where the mode sits in the game, and what all that asks of a comp.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-03

Ranked PvP against other players' defence teams. Unlocks at **AFK Stage 20**. See [game-modes.md](game-modes.md).

The comp this account fields is in [../account/teams.md](../account/teams.md). The rank it holds is in [../account/progression.md](../account/progression.md).

## Where it is

Inside the **Everlight**, the hub screen that holds every battle mode. See [game-modes.md](game-modes.md) for the walk.

Opening the Arena screen offers **3 random opponents**. Challenge one, or refresh the list. Refreshing is free **5 times a day**. `unverified`, one source.

## The rank ladder

Five named tiers, each split into three divisions, counted **I to III**. Arena Points decide the tier: win a fight and gain points, lose one and drop points.

| Tier | Arena Points |
| --- | --- |
| Novice I–III | 1,000 to 1,300 |
| Adept I–III | 1,300 to 1,750 |
| Elite I–III | 1,750 to 2,200 |
| Epic I–III | 2,200 to 2,650 |
| Legendary I–III | 2,650 to 3,000+ |

The point bands come from lootbar only. The tier names and their order are confirmed by two sources.

**The top of the ladder is `disputed`, and both readings are shown.** playafkjourney names a sixth tier, **Hall of Famer I–III**, and says players above **3,200 Arena Points** enter the Hall of Fame Rankings. lootbar instead ends the named tiers at Legendary III and puts a **Champion** bracket above it, the top 200 players, tallied daily, with a weekend Challenge Match for the top 100 and demotion back to Legendary III on a loss. Neither is checked against this account, which is nowhere near either.

Rewards are worked out from the tier held at **00:00 UTC every Monday**, and a one-off reward lands the first time a new tier is reached. Daily Arena coins buy hero shards in the Arena Store.

## The maps

**The account holds 3 Arena maps, and they carry different walls and tiles.** That is the account's own record, in [../account/progression.md](../account/progression.md), and it is the only Arena map fact this file states. **No source inside the freshness window covers Arena maps at all**, so everything else about them is a check to make in game rather than a claim to read here.

**Two things to settle in game, because they decide where the heroes stand.** Whether a saved Defence Team locks the map that every attacker fights you on, and whether more maps unlock as the Arena tier climbs. Both rested on 2024 pages only and have been cut. **One Arena screen settles both.**

**What the walls do is not in doubt**, and it is the part that decides fights: a **low wall blocks every hero from walking through it but lets ranged units shoot over it**. So the same five heroes are a different team on two different maps, and a melee carry is the hero a bad map punishes first. See [formation.md](formation.md).

**The practical rule needs none of that settled.** Read the map before locking your formation, and place for the walls on it rather than for the rows in the abstract.

## Attempts a day

**5 free challenge tickets a day, and up to 5 more for diamonds, so 10 attacks a day at most.** Prices climb with each ticket bought, and no source names the figures. See [diamonds.md](diamonds.md), which rates buying them worthwhile **only when a rank-up is within reach**.

## What it asks of a comp

Arena fights are short compared with a Legend Trial floor or an AFK Stage, so the matchup table in [team-archetypes.md](team-archetypes.md) points at **nuke and control**, not a scaling carry. Two things pull the other way on this account:

- **Levels do not pick the comp, but they can travel.** Every hero fields at the Resonance Level unless a Hand of Resonance slot is standing above it, and lending a slot is free. **Check [../account/progression.md](../account/progression.md) for whether the slots are level before drafting**: when one is ahead, give it to the carry or to the hero that has to survive. See [progression-systems.md](progression-systems.md).
- **You are fighting a defence team, not a boss.** Faction counter still pays its +15%, so the enemy faction is worth reading before each challenge. See [factions.md](factions.md).
