# Arena

> **Covers:** the Arena level baseline that deletes most of this account's investment, the defence team and its saved map, the battle timer and who wins it, the rank ladder, the daily limits, and what all that asks of a comp.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-09

Ranked PvP against other players' defence teams. Unlocks at **AFK Stage 20**. See [game-modes.md](game-modes.md).

**Arena is a non-season mode.** Season Resonance Level, Season Equipment, Season Magic Charms and Season Artifacts pay nothing here. That is why a permanent-track account can compete in it at all. See [seasons.md](seasons.md).

**Read [pvp-team-building.md](pvp-team-building.md) before drafting anything for this mode.** The general method in [team-building.md](team-building.md) is built for fights against the game, and three of its steps give the wrong answer here.

The comps this account fields are in [../account/teams.md](../account/teams.md). The rank it holds is in [../account/progression.md](../account/progression.md).

## The level baseline, which is the rule that decides everything else

**Arena sets every hero to a baseline level of 151.** A hero above 151 is pulled down to 151. A hero between the baseline and 240 has **10% of the baseline added**, so a maxed hero fights at about **166**. **Hero equipment levels are adjusted to match.** The baseline switches on once a player's Resonance Level reaches 151. Confirmed by two sources.

**Unlocked skills are not affected.** A skill a hero has, it keeps.

That single rule reorders the whole account.

| What the account has | What it is worth in Arena |
| --- | --- |
| Resonance Level 240 plus Resonance Synergy +25, so 265 | **Nothing.** Cut to about 166, the same as every opponent past 151 |
| Class Equipment at 240 on all thirty-six slots | **Nothing.** Adjusted down with the hero |
| Total power 22,636,000 | **Nothing.** It is a readout of the two rows above |
| **Ascension tier** | **Everything.** Skills are untouched, so a Supreme hero keeps skills an Epic hero does not have |
| **Faction bonus and the counter wheel** | **Full value.** Both are percentages of the capped stats |
| Hero kit, formation and the map | **Full value** |

**This explains the account's own losing record, and it is the account's evidence rather than a guide's.** Attacks lose to defence teams **50,000 power weaker**. Power was never the axis. Both sides fight at 166.

**So the only investment that buys Arena rank is ascension.** A Soul Sigil is worth more here than any number of levels, and the account's cheapest ascensions are exactly the heroes it fields in PvP. See [../account/progression.md](../account/progression.md).

**`unverified`: whether the baseline touches Exclusive Equipment.** No source states it. Two readings stand. The wording says *hero equipment levels* are matched to hero levels, and Class Equipment is the gear that carries a 1-to-240 level, so **EX weapons may be untouched**; against that, the same sentence may cover every worn item. It matters, because [../account/equipment.md](../account/equipment.md) prices five EX levels on Taichi & Agumon at **500 Temporal Essence** and calls them an Arena buy. **One Arena battle settles it: read Agumon's stat block on the formation screen and see whether the EX bonus is still at +10.**

## Attack and defence are two different jobs

**You hold one saved Defence Team, and other players attack it. You also attack theirs.** These are not the same team and must not be built from the same rules.

- **The attacker has to kill all five.** A fight that ends with anything of the defender's alive is a loss for the attacker.
- **The defender wins the clock.** If the timer runs out, the defence takes the win. So a defence does not need to kill anything. It needs to still be standing.

`unverified`: the length of the timer. One source outside the ranking window puts any AFK Journey fight at **90 seconds**, and no in-window source states a figure. The rule about who wins it is confirmed by two sources; the number is not.

## The map is yours, and you keep it

**The map saved with your Defence Team is the map every attacker fights you on. Maps are not randomised.** So the defence picks the terrain and the attacker reads it.

**More maps unlock as the Arena tier climbs.** The account holds **3**, which is its own record in [../account/progression.md](../account/progression.md).

**What the walls do decides fights.** A **low wall blocks every hero from walking through it but lets ranged units shoot over it**. So the same five heroes are a different team on two maps, and a melee carry is the hero a bad map punishes first. See [formation.md](formation.md).

Two rules follow, and they point in opposite directions.

- **On defence, pick the map that punishes a melee carry**, and place behind its low walls. The map is a sixth team member and it is the only one that never changes.
- **On attack, read the map before locking the formation.** It is the defender's choice, and it was chosen against you.

## Choosing who to fight

**Opening the Arena screen offers 3 random opponents, and the list refreshes free 5 times a day.** Selection reads Arena Points and overall power.

**Scout the defence before spending a ticket.** The formation is visible. This is the half of the mode the account is not using: three offers plus five refreshes is **eighteen defences seen for five attacks**, so the fight is chosen rather than endured. See [pvp-team-building.md](pvp-team-building.md).

## Attempts a day

**5 free challenge tickets a day, and up to 5 more for diamonds, so 10 attacks a day at most.** Prices climb with each ticket bought, and no source names the figures. See [diamonds.md](diamonds.md), which rates buying them worthwhile **only when a rank-up is within reach**.

**Spend all of them.** Points lost to an occasional loss are outweighed by the wins taken elsewhere on the ladder.

## The rank ladder

Five named tiers, each split into three divisions, counted **I to III**. Arena Points decide the tier: win a fight and gain points, lose one and drop points.

| Tier | Arena Points |
| --- | --- |
| Novice I–III | 1,000 to 1,300 |
| Adept I–III | 1,300 to 1,750 |
| Elite I–III | 1,750 to 2,200 |
| Epic I–III | 2,200 to 2,650 |
| Legendary I–III | 2,650 to 3,000+ |

The point bands come from lootbar only. The tier names and their order are confirmed by two sources.

**The top of the ladder is `disputed`, and both readings are shown.** playafkjourney names a sixth tier, **Hall of Famer I–III**, and says players above **3,200 Arena Points** enter the Hall of Fame Rankings. lootbar instead ends the named tiers at Legendary III and puts a **Champion** bracket above it, the top 200 players, tallied daily, with a weekend Challenge Match for the top 100 and demotion back to Legendary III on a loss. Neither is checked against this account, which is nowhere near either.

Rewards are worked out from the tier held at **00:00 UTC every Monday**, and a one-off reward lands the first time a new tier is reached. Daily Arena coins buy hero shards in the Arena Store, and the higher tiers pay Exclusive Equipment upgrade materials.
