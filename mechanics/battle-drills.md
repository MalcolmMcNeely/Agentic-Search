# Battle Drills

> **Covers:** the guild map mode: the weekly round, the four location types, stamina, the three difficulty modes, Endless Mode and the one-deploy-a-day rule, and what all that asks of a comp.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-01

A guild mode, not a solo one. The guild works one map together, and **progress on every enemy piece is shared**, so one member finishing a piece another member started still counts.

## The round

A new round opens at **00:00 UTC every Tuesday**. Each round is a map of several areas plus **one final boss**. The guild starts at base camp and can only challenge areas connected to what it already holds. Clear every enemy piece in an area to capture it. Capture enough pieces and the final boss unlocks.

Join a round and you are tied to that guild for it. Switching guilds mid-round keeps your personal rewards but stops you contributing to the new guild.

## The four location types

| Location | What it is | Why it matters to a comp |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camp | the starting tiles, each carrying a **faction-specific buff** | a mono-faction team cashes a camp buff in full, a mixed one does not. Camps cost no stamina |
| Passage | a multi-stage fight, rated Easy, Regular or Hard | several fights back to back, so it wants sustain over burst |
| Elite Stronghold | one strong enemy set | clearing it grants a **skill bonus to the whole guild the next day**, inside Battle Drills only |
| Boss Stronghold | the mid-boss and the final boss | one health bar, and performance is scored on **damage dealt** |

## Stamina

Each hero starts a round with **120 stamina** and spends **40 a battle**, so a hero is good for **3 battles**. Camps cost nothing.

`unverified`: whether the stamina above and the "Action Points" other sources name are one resource or two. The wiki says members spend Action Points to challenge pieces and gives no figures; Talk Android gives the 120 and 40 and calls it stamina. Both readings are recorded rather than merged.

## The three modes, in order

1. **Stout Recruit.** The entry mode. Beat its final boss to open the next.
2. **Seasoned Challenger.** The same map again, but every buff, camp skill and camp level won in Stout Recruit carries over.
3. **Restless Warrior.** The final boss respawns each time it dies, until the round ends.

Beating the final boss also makes the **next** round harder.

## Endless Mode, and the rule that shapes the roster

Endless Mode opens once the final boss falls, and escalates through **5 difficulty levels**.

**In Endless Mode each hero can be deployed only once a day.** That is the rule that decides how to build here. A hero spent on a passage is gone from the boss fight for the rest of the day, so the mode is answered by **roster depth**, not by one stacked team. See [../account/teams.md](../account/teams.md).

## What to build

- **Bring several teams that share no heroes.** One team, however strong, clears one fight a day in Endless.
- **Every team needs a Tank, a damage dealer and a Support.** That is the floor, not the target.
- **At a Boss Stronghold, favour single-target damage and cut the tanks back**, because the score there is damage dealt rather than survival.
- **Build each team on one faction where the roster allows it**, so a camp buff lands on all five. See [factions.md](factions.md).
