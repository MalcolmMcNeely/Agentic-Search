# Solo battles

> **Covers:** the single-hero encounters on the World Map and in the story, and which hero traits win them.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

A solo battle is a World Map or story encounter fought with **one hero**, not a team of five. The hero picked gets buffs set by that encounter, and the buffs differ from one solo battle to the next.

`unverified`. One source only. Alucare describes it as "facing enemies with a single champion of your choice, this hero will benefit from special and different augmentations depending on the solitary event". No second guide covers the encounter type at all, and the fandom wiki, prydwen and playafkjourney battle-mode pages do not name it. The hero-count rule and the per-encounter buffs both rest on that one source.

Alucare lists four other World Map encounter types beside it: normal fights at zone level, elite fights against one high-HP monster, wave fights, and tactical exercises that hand you set heroes to teach a mechanic. Only the solo battle restricts the count to one.

## What this changes about hero choice

Every rule in [team-building.md](team-building.md) that turns on other heroes is dead here.

- **The faction bonus pays nothing.** It needs 3, 4 or 5 same-faction heroes deployed. One hero clears no threshold. See [factions.md](factions.md). The faction **counter wheel** still pays, because it is a matchup between the deployed hero and the enemy, not a team count.
- **Every ally-facing line in a kit is wasted.** A shield handed to an ally, an ATK buff on an adjacent ally, a team heal, a taunt that protects a backline: none of it lands. Read the kit and count only what the hero does to itself and to the enemy.
- **Summons are the exception.** A hero who summons still gets a second body on the field. Summoners keep more of their kit than buffers do.
- **A carry with no healer behind it has to heal itself.** The fight is attrition with no support, so self-heal, self-shield and lifesteal decide it, the same logic as the sustain archetype in [team-archetypes.md](team-archetypes.md).
- **Range still matters.** No tank stands in front, so a range 1 hero eats every hit from the first second. See [formation.md](formation.md).

The hero to bring is therefore the one whose whole kit points inward: its own damage, its own healing, its own shield, and a summon if it has one.
