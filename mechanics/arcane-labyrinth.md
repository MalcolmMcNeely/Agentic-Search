# Arcane Labyrinth

> **Covers:** how the Arcane Labyrinth run works, what carries between fights, relics and crests, and what that demands of a team.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

A roguelike dungeon. It is the one mode where the fight does not start fresh, so it is built differently from every other comp in this repo.

## The run

| Thing | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Unlocks at | AFK Stage 100 |
| Heroes picked at the start | 10 |
| Heroes fielded per fight | 5 |
| Floors | 160 |
| Floors per difficulty | 20 |
| Relic slots per run | 4 `unverified`, one source |
| Exploration attempts | 3 per refresh, plus 5 more after a first clear of floor 20 |

**The one rule that changes everything: HP and Energy do not reset between battles.** Buffs carry too. A hero that ends floor 3 at 20% HP starts floor 4 at 20% HP. When a hero dies it is gone for the run, and a bench hero takes the slot. The run ends when the bench runs out.

Dead enemies do not come back either. A retry only fields the enemies that survived.

Each floor offers two doors. Each door is a different fight and a different relic reward.

Exploration rewards land on clearing floors 5, 10 and 15. Floor 15 is followed by an Elite Challenge Boss. You can leave mid-run and keep what you earned.

## Relics and crests

A **relic** is a run-only buff, handed out after each win: team stats up, enemies weakened, or a special ability. Each relic has 2 stages, and a second copy upgrades the buff.

A **crest** is a passive unlocked by holding 2, 4, 6 or 8 relics of the same type. Stacking one relic type beats spreading across four.

Guides name **Warmth** (HP recovery), **Fortitude** (team HP) and **Guard** (defence) as the best picks. All three feed the same thing: surviving the next floor with HP left.

## Pure Crystals and Fitz's Store

**Pure Crystals** are the run currency. Fitz's Store appears mid-run and before a final boss, and sells relic shards, items and Resurrection Potions. 20 crystals rerolls a crest choice.

## Deep Labyrinth

After floor 20 is cleared, an endless boss mode opens. Ranking is by damage dealt to the final boss, the **Mistal Berserker**. No clear rewards, leaderboard rewards only.

## What this demands of a team

Read with [team-building.md](team-building.md) and [team-archetypes.md](team-archetypes.md).

1. **Healing is the whole mode.** Everywhere else a healer keeps heroes alive for one fight. Here healing is stored, because the HP it restores is carried into the next floor. Over-heal is not waste, it is fuel.
2. **Bring a bench, not a team.** 10 slots. Slots 6 to 10 are a second tank, a third healer, and bodies to spend on early floors. Guides advise fielding weaker heroes early to keep the strong ones fresh.
3. **No counter-pick.** The enemy faction changes floor to floor, so the +15% counter bonus in [factions.md](factions.md) cannot be planned around. Take the biggest same-faction bonus instead.
4. **Attrition, not burst.** Scaling carry and sustain-with-offence beat nuke here. See [team-archetypes.md](team-archetypes.md).
5. **Pick one relic type and stack it,** for the crest.

**Role split is `unverified`.** gamerant says at least **two healers**, one tank, one DPS. afk.guide says at least **one** healer, one tank, one DPS, with slots 4 and 5 flexible. Both shown, not averaged. The HP-carryover rule points at gamerant's reading.
