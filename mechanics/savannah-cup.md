# Savannah Cup

> **Covers:** the live 3v3 real-time PvP event, its ban-and-draft phase, the equal-progression rule that makes it the fairest mode in the game for this account, the hidden formation, and how to play the draft.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-09

A **real-time 3v3 PvP** event, running in version **1.7.4**, from **3 September 2026** to the middle of the month. It follows the earlier Solstice Clash format. `?` the exact end date and the unlock requirement; neither is stated by any source found, and the in-game calendar carries both.

**This is the one PvP mode where the account's roster does not matter and its investment cannot be out-spent.** Two rules do that.

- **Every hero in the event is set to the same progression level.** No ascension advantage, no levels, no equipment.
- **You may deploy heroes you do not own.** The mode supplies the pool.

So nothing in `account/` decides a pick here. The skill in the mode is the draft and the placement. Compare [honor-duel.md](honor-duel.md), which equalises the same way.

## The draft

1. The system draws **20 heroes at random** from the pool for the current session.
2. **Each side bans 1 hero.**
3. Both sides take turns drafting **3 unique heroes each** from what is left.

**Matchmaking reads ranking points**, so opponents are near your own standing.

**Depth beats a single strong lineup.** You cannot bring a prepared team. You get 20 heroes you did not choose, minus two bans, and you draft into that. Knowing what many heroes do is the whole skill.

## The battle

- **Neither side sees the other's formation during the preparation phase.** Hero positions stay hidden until the battle starts. So there is no counter-placement, unlike the two-stage reveal in [honor-duel.md](honor-duel.md).
- **Leaving before the formation phase ends is an automatic loss.**

## How to play it

The general shapes in [pvp-team-building.md](pvp-team-building.md) carry over. Everything about investment does not.

- **Ban the hero that wins alone.** With only three slots a side, one hero that hard-counters a whole shape is worth more than a marginal upgrade to your own draft.
- **Draft for coverage, not for a combo.** Three heroes cannot afford a piece that needs a fourth to work.
- **A ramp hero is a poor pick.** Three-a-side fights are short, and there is no frontline deep enough to buy a long wind-up. See [hero-scoring.md](hero-scoring.md).
- **Take the sustain slot.** With three heroes, losing one is losing a third of the team, so a heal or a mitigation is a larger share of the fight than it is at five.
- **The formation is hidden, so place for the map and for a fight you cannot see.** Do not build a placement that only works against one enemy shape.
- **Score every hero the pool offers on its kit at the table.** The pool is drawn fresh, and no tier list ranks anything for this mode. See [hero-scoring.md](hero-scoring.md).

## Rewards

Servers are grouped into **Districts** by active player count. Three ranking boards run at once: **District Points**, **District Guesses** and **Cross-Server Guesses**, the last resetting daily. The Guesses boards are a spectator bet on other matches rather than a record of your own play, so **rewards are reachable without winning any fight**.

Top 1-20 on District Points pays a **Solstice Champion** frame. Top 1-50 on District Guesses pays a **Master of Odds** title.
