# Seasons

> **Covers:** the two parallel progression tracks, exactly what a season reset wipes and what survives it, the settlement payout, the season gate, Season 7 Crown of Ashes and Season 8 Veil of Memories.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-10

## Two tracks, not one

AFK Journey runs **permanent** progression and **seasonal** progression side by side. A comp built from the wrong track is wrong.

| Permanent (Starter Story) | Seasonal |
| --- | --- |
| Hero ascension tiers | Season Resonance Level |
| Resonance Level and Resonance Synergy | Season Equipment |
| Class Equipment | Season Artifacts |
| Exclusive Equipment | Magic Charms |
| Non-season artifacts | Soul Pact / Phantimals |
| | Season AFK Stage |

**Season access is Resonance Level 240 plus the story quest `Ryeham Tales: New Journey`.** A quest then appears in Holistone pointing at the season region. The two names the sources give are two halves of one quest name, not two quests: the wiki carries a quest page titled `Ryeham Tales: New Journey`, the Season page shortens it to `Ryeham Tales`, and the 1.7.1 patch notes shorten it to `New Journey`.

## What resets and what survives

This is the question that decides whether a season is worth playing, so it is itemised rather than summarised.

**Survives, untouched, into every future season:** hero ascension tiers, permanent Resonance Level, Resonance Synergy, Class Equipment, Exclusive Equipment, non-season artifacts, and every permanent currency — Diamonds, all three kinds of Invite Letter, Temporal Essence, Stellar Crystals, Hero Essence, Training Manuals, Faction and Omni-Acorns, Soul Sigils, Season Tokens.

**Heroes recruited or ascended during a season keep their tier.** A hero is never un-owned at a reset. This is the most important fact about seasons: **pulling and ascending during a season is permanent progress**, whatever else the season wipes.

**Wiped at season end:** Season Resonance Level and its Season Essence and Season Manuals, Season Equipment (deleted from the bag), Season Artifacts, Magic Charms and their crafting inputs (Golden, Crimson and Branco Charm Essence, Radiant Crests), Soul Pact and Phantimals, the Season AFK Stage, every seasonal-mode ranking, and **unused Season Milestone points and unopened Milestone chests**.

**Nothing converts directly.** Season gear does not become permanent gear, and season currency is not refunded. There are exactly two routes by which a season pays out in permanent goods, and both are below.

## The settlement

**A season pays a settlement, and it is mailed when the *next* season begins, not when the current one ends.** It scales on how far four things got: Season Resonance Level, Season Equipment, Season Artifacts and Season Magic Charms.

`unverified`: no in-window source itemises what a settlement actually pays. The often-quoted figures, 18,000 points for a Celestial or Hypogean Selector and 22,000 for an Exclusive Equipment Reset, trace to a single 2024-12-20 page outside the 12-month window and are not carried here. **Season 7's own table is unpublished**; the wiki says the settlement rules are released before the season ends. Read it off the in-game **Season Handbook**.

## The Season Store is the other payout route

**Season Tokens buy permanent goods**, and that store is not season-only. See [season-store.md](season-store.md).

## Skipping a season costs the season-only rewards and nothing else

Seasons are designed to start everyone level, and a finished season stays playable in [peaks-of-time.md](peaks-of-time.md). An account that skips one falls behind on that season's rewards, not on its permanent progression.

`unverified`: no usable community account of the Season 6 to Season 7 transition was found. Reddit blocks the crawler, so this is an access failure rather than evidence of silence.

## Entering late is subsidised

**A catch-up buff scales with how many days the season has already run on the server.** Entering on day one is not assisted; entering late is. So a late entry climbs the Season AFK Stage ladder faster per fight than a day-one player did.

## Season 7: Crown of Ashes

Runs 2026-05-29 to **2026-09-23**. Version 1.7.4, released 2026-09-02, is the final phase.

Three phases. Each raises the Season Resonance Level cap and the Season AFK Stage challenge cap, and each uses its own upgrade items.

`unverified`: the per-phase cap numbers. The only sourced ceiling datum is that the designers reduced the effect of **Season Resonance above 560** in Supreme Arena and Season Dream Realm, so the track runs into the high hundreds.

New in this season:

- **Soul Pact**, which replaces Faction Talents. See below.
- Season-exclusive Legendary artifacts. Breakthrough Spell and Arc Burst Spell come from Story Quests early. Sunlance Spell and Magicsurge Spell come from Season Milestones and are the endgame picks. `unverified`: the count and the level cap. Sources say 8, 10 and 12 artifacts, and +25 or +30 for the cap.
- A new Magic Charm set, crafted and refined from Essence and Crests.

## Season 8: Veil of Memories

**Starts 2026-09-23 at 15:00 UTC+8, on version 1.8.1**, the moment Crown of Ashes ends. Set in **Novaris, a Graveborn city**.

Four new heroes: **Estrilda, Thane, Senea, Aster**.

**A Season 8 Preview Event runs 2026-09-09 to 2026-09-13.** It pays 2 Invite Letters, 2 Rate Up Invite Letters, 2 Epic Invite Letters and 6 Origami Hamsters across three tiers, one of which claims only after the season begins.

**Karma's Rate Up runs 2026-09-17 to 2026-10-07**, straddling the changeover.

**A season start does not hand out free hero copies.** The free Supreme+ Aurora set was a one-off at Season 6. The Season 7 start mail carried essences and EX Equipment Resets and no copies at all. Do not bank diamonds expecting a giveaway.

`?` whether Soul Pact and Phantimals return in Veil of Memories or are replaced by a new seasonal mechanic. No source states it.

## Soul Pact and Phantimals

Five Phantimals, one per faction group: Lightbearer, Wilder, Mauler, Graveborn, and Celestial & Hypogean together.

**Deploy three heroes of one faction and that faction's Phantimal unlocks for the battle.** This is a second, independent reason to run a 3-hero faction core, on top of the stat bonus in [factions.md](factions.md).

A Phantimal's stats come from the levels and tiers of the heroes deployed. Phantimals level up through AFK stages and unlock or strengthen skills as they go. Each can put a **Spirit Mark** on one deployed ally for a unique buff.

Soul Pact is free and resets at season end.
