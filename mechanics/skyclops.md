# Skyclops

> **Covers:** the Skyclops Dream Realm boss, its Wings mechanic, the damage types that beat it and the artifact to bring.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-30

One of the four Dream Realm bosses. Dream Realm is a damage race against a timer, so the score is damage dealt, not a kill. See [game-modes.md](game-modes.md).

Skyclops is the boss that punishes a one-damage-type team. Everything below follows from that.

## Faction

A **Mage of the "Other" faction at base range 20**, so it reaches any tile on the grid. `unverified`, one source. Either way the counter wheel in [factions.md](factions.md) has no entry for it, so pick heroes on kit and damage type, not on faction advantage. Same call as [snow-stomper.md](snow-stomper.md).

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Opener | Targets the hero closest to it |
| Multi-attack | Deals physical and magical damage together, each raised 15% while the matching Wings are on the field |
| Summon Wings of Light | Wings that cut the **physical** damage Skyclops takes |
| Summon Wings of Darkness | Wings that cut the **magical** damage Skyclops takes |
| Sweeping laser (Ultimate) | Sweeps a 10-tile arc across the party for 260% damage to every hero it grazes |

Wings inherit **40% of Skyclops' ATK and 90% of its HP**, so they are not chip damage.

`unverified`, sources disagree, both readings kept:

- **How many Wings.** destructoid and gfinityesports say **four** per summon. The Prydwen summary says **one of each type**. The fight is described as 3 to 4 summon waves either way.
- **How hard the resistance bites.** gfinityesports gives **40% at boss level 1 rising to 90% at level 6**. dotesports and sportskeeda give a flat **90%**.
- **Whether the boss is untargetable.** destructoid and the Prydwen summary say Skyclops holds **damage immunity** while any Wing lives. Other guides describe resistance only, not immunity.

Under every reading the same play wins: **kill the Wings first**, then hit the boss.

## Team shape

Five roles, agreed across guides: tank, debuffer, buffer, healer and a **DPS that hits more than one target**. The AoE requirement is what separates this boss from Snow Stomper, which is a pure single-target race.

Two rules matter more than the role list:

1. **Bring both damage types.** The Wings flip which defence is stacked. A team that is all magic or all physical spends half the fight resisted.
2. **True damage ignores the flip entirely.** It is not reduced by either resistance, so it is the only output that never stops paying.

## Artifact

**Starshard Spell**, named by every guide that names one. Its wave is true damage, which is exactly what the Wings cannot stop.
