# Season progression

> **Covers:** how the seasonal track is climbed, what feeds Season Resonance Level, Season Equipment, the season gates and their unlock rules, and the order to do them in during a first season week.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-10

What resets and what survives is in [seasons.md](seasons.md). This file is the climb itself.

## Season Resonance Level is a second, parallel Resonating Hall

It runs the same rules as the permanent one — five Hands, a 10-level spread, the lowest Hand setting the level — and none of the same fuel. See [progression-systems.md](progression-systems.md).

- **It is fed by Season Essence and Season Manuals, and those drop only from Season AFK Stages.** No permanent currency buys a season level, and no season currency buys a permanent one. The two tracks never touch.
- **A permanent Resonance Level 240 buys no head start.** Everyone enters a season level. That is the design intent, not an oversight.
- **It is capped per phase.** Each season phase raises the cap and changes which upgrade items feed it.

**So the Season AFK Stage push is the only faucet, and everything else in the season is downstream of it.**

## Season Equipment is a separate parallel set

Forged in-season, usable only while the season is live, and deleted from the bag at season end. **It does not replace permanent Class Equipment and permanent Class Equipment does not substitute for it.**

**The level of equipment that can be forged is tied to the Season Resonance Level**, so the two climb together and neither runs ahead.

Since 1.7.4, AFK Stage clears pay **Select Equipment Chests**, which let the piece be chosen, rather than dropping at random.

## The gates

| Gate | Unlock | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Supreme Arena** | Season AFK Stage **130** | Confirmed. See [supreme-arena.md](supreme-arena.md) |
| **Dura's Trials**, the only source of Magic Charms | the **9th day after the season starts on the server**, recommended at Season Resonance **190+** | Confirmed on the wiki's own page |

**Dura's Trials is gated on the calendar, not on effort.** The figure of Season AFK Stage 305 that circulated for it traces only to a banned source and appears nowhere on the wiki. It is rejected. See [tier-list-sources.md](tier-list-sources.md).

**That difference matters for planning.** A stage gate can be rushed; a day-9 gate cannot. An account entering a season with fewer than 9 days left on the clock will not see Magic Charms in that season at all.

No other Season AFK Stage gate was found.

## The order for a first season week

Each step feeds the one under it, so this is a dependency chain rather than a preference list.

1. **Push Season AFK Stages.** It is the sole source of Season Essence and Season Manuals, and every gate below reads a season stage or a season level.
2. **Spend on Season Resonance Level.** It gates what equipment can be forged.
3. **Forge Season Equipment**, choosing pieces from the Select Equipment Chests.
4. **Unlock and upgrade Soul Pact / Phantimals.** It is free, its materials are front-loaded into each phase, and it rewards the 3-hero faction core the comps already run. See [seasons.md](seasons.md) and [factions.md](factions.md).
5. **Season Artifacts.** The highest-ceiling seasonal system, but drip-fed by version updates rather than by effort, so it cannot be rushed.
6. **Dura's Trials and Magic Charms**, once day 9 has passed.
7. **Supreme Arena**, once Season AFK Stage 130 is behind you.

## What the season does not change

**The season uses the permanent roster and permanent ascension tiers.** Hero tiers and Exclusive Equipment carry in. So every ascension bought outside a season is worth the same inside one, and the hero-scoring and team-building method is unchanged. See [hero-scoring.md](hero-scoring.md) and [team-building.md](team-building.md).
