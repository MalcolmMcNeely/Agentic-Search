# Peaks of Time

> **Covers:** what Peaks of Time is, which past seasons sit in it, what it pays, the gate that locks it, and why this account cannot open it yet.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-31

**Peaks of Time is the archive of finished seasons. It is not a puzzle mode and it is not a dungeon.** A season ends, its region and its exclusive modes move into Peaks of Time, and the account can walk back in and play the storyline it missed.

**Do not read AFK Arena guides for this.** AFK Arena has a mode with the same name that is a treasure-chest puzzle map paying artifacts and Hero's Essence. It is a different feature in a different game. Every search on this term returns the AFK Arena version first.

## What sits in it

Six finished seasons: **Song of Strife, Waves of Intrigue, Chains of Eternity, Echoes of Dissent, Thorns of Devotion, Tower of Memory.** Those are Seasons 1 to 6.

**Season 7, Crown of Ashes, is live and therefore not in here yet.** It started 2026-05-29 and joins Peaks of Time when it ends. See [seasons.md](seasons.md).

## What it pays

**The season's own content, replayed.** Walking a past season's map pays its map resources, its story quest rewards and its exclusive modes.

**Progress carries over.** An account that played a season part way resumes at the point it stopped, rather than starting again.

`unverified`: no in-window source lists the exact reward table for a Peaks of Time run. Sources describe it as "plenty of map resources" and the season storyline, and go no further.

**What it does not pay is the season's live rewards.** Season Resonance Level, Season Equipment, Season Artifacts, Magic Charms, Soul Pact and Phantimals are the seasonal track, and that track resets at season end. Peaks of Time returns the story and the map, not the ladder. See [seasons.md](seasons.md).

## The gate

**Peaks of Time sits behind season access, and season access needs Resonance Level 240.** A quest then appears in Holistone pointing at the season region.

`unverified`, two readings of the same wiki page name different story quests as the second half of that gate: **Ryeham Tales** in one, **New Journey** in the other. Both agree on Resonance Level 240.

`unverified`, and standing on a source dated 2024-09-17, outside the 12-month window, with nothing newer confirming it: reaching a *missed* season inside Peaks of Time additionally needs the quest **The Last Leg**.

## What this means for this account

**Peaks of Time is locked, and the gate is a long way off.** The account holds Resonance Level 180 against a gate of 240, and its story sits at Merlin's Ward. See [../account/progression.md](../account/progression.md).

**So it is not worth doing, because it cannot be done.** The work that opens it is the same work already named as the account's bottleneck: levels into Rolan raise the Resonance Level, and story progress is separately the last blocker on Trial of Abyss.

**Nothing is expiring.** Peaks of Time is permanent and the six archived seasons do not rotate out, so nothing is being lost by reaching it late.

**This settles the open Magic Charm question.** Dura's Trials, the source of all Magic Charms, needs **Season** AFK Stage 305. Season access needs Resonance Level 240 and the account holds 180, so the account cannot be on the season ladder at all. AFK Stage 1142 is the permanent ladder, and charms are not available.
