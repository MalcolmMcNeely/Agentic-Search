# Damian

> **Covers:** Damian kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-08

- **Faction:** Wilder
- **Class:** Support
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 20
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

**Ultimate, Explode, My Chariot!** At battle start he deploys a **Toy Chariot** in his place, which inherits his stats and a share of his HP. **While the Chariot stands, Damian himself cannot be attacked.** The Ultimate charges the Chariot into the enemy line, detonates it, and **blinds** the enemies it hits.

**Skill I, Emergency Support!** The Chariot heals the weakest ally within 3 tiles for **120% HP, four times**, or returns Energy to Damian instead.

**Skill II, It's a Plane!** A toy plane attacks the **farthest** enemy, damaging it and **stunning it for 3s**. That is reach the rest of the roster does not have: it hits the enemy backline directly rather than working forward through the front.

## Tier gates

**He stands at Elite, the tier an A-Level hero unlocks at, so all three ascension pieces are dark.** See [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).

| Piece | Gate | Live at Elite |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explode, My Chariot! | none | yes |
| Emergency Support! | none | yes |
| It's a Plane! | none | yes |
| Hero Focus | Legendary+ | **no** |
| Inventor's Will | Mythic+ | **no** |
| Enhance Force | Supreme+ | **no** |

**Hero Focus** adds 12 to 20% ATK. **Inventor's Will is his Exclusive Equipment** and hands allies Haste and healing, with further steps at EX +5, +10 and +15. **Enhance Force** stretches the blind to 7s while the Chariot is above half HP.

**Elite to Legendary+ is 21 Soul Sigils** for an A-Level hero, the same price as Viperian and Niru. **The Dream Store stocks him**, so those copies are a Dream Fragment purchase rather than a banner pull. He queues behind Antandra. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md).

## Best for

**Untouchable backline support, and enemy-backline control.** The Chariot is the only "cannot be attacked" clause on the roster, and It's a Plane! is the only skill that reaches the farthest enemy on purpose. Both are aimed at the exact failure the push comp reports: a backline deleted inside 10 seconds. See [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md).

**Do not field him at Elite and expect the guide hero.** Guides describe him at **Mythic+ with the EX weapon at +5**, acting as a smaller Rowan. Below that gate he is a Chariot, one heal and one stun, which is a slow-grind pick and not a burst-race one. See [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md).

**Range 20 puts him in the back row**, and the Chariot stands where he would have. See [../formation.md](../formation.md).

## Notes

**Wilder is 10 deep with him in it**, and Tower of Nature is Wilder-locked at 55 of 90, so he has a faction-locked mode to grow into. See [../legend-trial.md](../legend-trial.md).
