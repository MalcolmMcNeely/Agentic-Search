# Daimon

> **Covers:** Daimon kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-30

- **Faction:** Graveborn
- **Class:** Tank
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 3
- **Rarity:** A `unverified`

## Kit

Ultimate sends his guardian spirit Stitchy to slash enemies for true damage scaled to target max HP, and shields allies. Core skills build shields through drain and hand them to bonded allies while cutting incoming damage.

## Best for

Campaign and PvP. A damage soak and shield engine, and the class label agrees: he is a Tank.

## Notes

**`disputed`: afk.global lists Daimon as a Warrior. The game lists him as a Tank.** The account wins, per the rule in [../../CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md). This is not cosmetic. Class Equipment is shared per class, so Daimon draws on **Tank** gear, which sits at 226 on this account, not **Warrior** gear at 230. That costs him 3 levels of gear and buys the correct role and stat block. See [../../account/equipment.md](../../account/equipment.md).

**`unverified`: his Level.** Two readings that do not reconcile.

- **A-Level.** afk.global lists his Rarity as **Elite**, and A-Level heroes start at Elite while S-Level heroes start at Epic. See [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md).
- **S-Level.** The Fandom wiki and prydwen both describe him as S-Level.

afk.global is the weaker source, because the same page gets his class wrong. But it is the only one that states a starting tier rather than a tier-list letter, and tier-list letters are routinely mistaken for rarity.

It matters because the Dream Store stocks no S-Level hero, so his Level decides whether copies of him can be bought there. He is absent from the live shelf, but so is Bonnie, who is A-Level on every reading, so the shelf proves nothing on its own. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md).
