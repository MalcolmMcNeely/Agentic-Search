# Daimon

> **Covers:** Daimon kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

- **Faction:** Graveborn
- **Class:** Warrior
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 3
- **Level:** A

## Kit

Ultimate sends his guardian spirit Stitchy to slash enemies for true damage scaled to target max HP, and shields allies. Core skills build shields through drain and hand them to bonded allies while cutting incoming damage.

## Best for

Campaign and PvP. Plays as a damage soak and shield engine despite the Warrior label.
