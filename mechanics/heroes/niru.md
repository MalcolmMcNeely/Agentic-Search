# Niru

> **Covers:** Niru kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-04

- **Faction:** Graveborn
- **Class:** Support
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 1
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

Soul Shepherd is the Ultimate and the reason to field him. It watches the weakest ally, and when a fatal blow lands it turns that ally into a **Spirit** instead of a corpse, restoring them to **45% of max HP**. A Spirit cannot be attacked at all. It deals **45%** of its normal damage and loses a rising amount of HP every second, so it is a stay of execution rather than a revive.

Spirit Devour drains every enemy at once for **150% damage plus 3% of the target's max HP, capped at 70% of his ATK**, then heals the weakest ally for the same amount. Soul Reaping hits the weakest enemy and pays more the more HP that enemy has already lost.

`unverified`: the Soul Reaping cap. afk.global gives 0.3 times the target's lost HP up to **450%**, and no second in-window source states a figure.

He is melee, range 1, which is unusual for a Support and is a formation constraint rather than a kit one. See [../formation.md](../formation.md).

## Best for

**He is a cheat-death, and the roster had none.** Every other survival tool here arrives before the hit: a shield, a taunt, damage reduction, invincibility. Soul Shepherd fires after it, so it covers the one thing mitigation cannot, a burst larger than the mitigation. See [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md).

**Spirit Devour is percentage-of-max-HP damage, and it heals off the same hit.** The percentage part ignores enemy defence, which is the damage type an uncapped ladder cannot resist, and it lands on every enemy. The cap at 70% of his ATK is what holds it back, so he is a healer that chips rather than a damage dealer. See [../../account/progression.md](../../account/progression.md).

**Campaign** `unverified`: rated **A+** [afk.global character page, ratings undated, investment not stated] and **B** in story mode [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated].

**Dream Realm** `unverified`: rated **C** as Boss [afk.global character page, ratings undated, investment not stated] and **D** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. Both readings agree it is his worst mode. Spirit Devour pays per enemy and a boss is one enemy.

**PvP** `unverified`: rated **A** [afk.global character page, ratings undated, investment not stated] and **B** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. The higher reading is the cheat-death: Arena is settled by which side loses a hero first.

**allclash does not name him at all** [2026-08-26]. That is scope, not a verdict. See the guide silence rule in [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md).

**The afk.global letters above come from his character page, not from the mode tabs of its tier list**, and that page states no date for them. They are shown because they name a mode, and they are marked `unverified` for the same reason. See [../tier-list-sources.md](../tier-list-sources.md).

## Tier gate

**Three of his skills are locked on this account.** He is **Elite**, the lowest ascension tier.

- **Hero Focus**, +17% HP for the whole battle, needs **Legendary+**, five steps up.
- **Ethereal Form** casts the Ultimate on the nearest ally at battle start for no Energy, and stops Spirits losing HP for a while. It is an **Exclusive Equipment gated at Mythic+**, seven steps up.
- **Enhance Force** stops a Soul Reaping target recovering HP for 8s. It needs **Supreme+**.

**Ethereal Form is the one that matters, and it is the one furthest away.** Without it Soul Shepherd waits on Energy, so an early burst kills the ally before the Ultimate is ready. So he protects against a slow grind now, and against opening burst only at Mythic+. See [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md) and [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).

He is **A-Level**, so the Dream Store stocks his copies. Elite to Legendary+ is 1 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 8 = **21 Soul Sigils**. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md).
