# Rolan

> **Covers:** Rolan kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

- **Faction:** Celestial
- **Class:** Support
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 10
- **Level:** S

## Kit

**Ultimate clone.** When an ally casts their Ultimate, a radiant clone appears, inherits 80% of that ally's base stats, and casts the same Ultimate again.

**Morale opener.** At battle start the team gains **6% Morale per hero** of the most-deployed faction. If that faction is **Lightbearer or Celestial the bonus rises to 10% per hero**. Rolan and those heroes also gain 20 Penetration and take 50% less damage for 8s.

The rest of his kit is healing, cleanse, team buffs and battlefield control, all keyed to Morale. Higher Morale unlocks his stronger effects, including Radiant Reign.

## Best for

PvE, AFK Stages and Dream Realm. Long fights, where Morale has time to climb. He is an **engine, not a carry**: he doubles someone else's Ultimate. See [../team-archetypes.md](../team-archetypes.md).

Named partners: Elijah & Lailah, Baelran, Galahad, Tasi. Galahad works as the frontline that protects Morale.

## Notes

Celestial, so he counts as any faction for the faction bonus, and adds +1% ATK and HP to the team just by being deployed. See [../factions.md](../factions.md).

Two 2026 guides call him the first Stargaze Station target, ahead of Elijah & Lailah, partly because he needs few copies to function. See [../recruitment.md](../recruitment.md).
