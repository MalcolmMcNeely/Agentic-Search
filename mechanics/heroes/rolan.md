# Rolan

> **Covers:** Rolan kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

- **Faction:** Celestial
- **Class:** Support
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 10
- **Rarity:** S

## Kit

**Ultimate clone.** When an ally casts their Ultimate, a radiant clone appears, inherits 80% of that ally's base stats, and casts the same Ultimate again.

**Morale opener.** At battle start the team gains **6% Morale per hero** of the most-deployed faction. If that faction is **Lightbearer or Celestial the bonus rises to 10% per hero**. Rolan and those heroes also gain 20 Penetration and take 50% less damage for 8s.

The rest of his kit is healing, cleanse, team buffs and battlefield control, all keyed to Morale. Higher Morale unlocks his stronger effects, including Radiant Reign.

## Best for

PvE, AFK Stages and Dream Realm. Long fights, where Morale has time to climb. He is an **engine, not a carry**: he doubles someone else's Ultimate. See [../team-archetypes.md](../team-archetypes.md).

Named partners: Elijah & Lailah, Baelran, Galahad, Tasi.

**The guides call Galahad his frontline. She is not one here.** She is a Mage at range 10, so she stands in the back row beside him. The pairing still works, because he clones her Ultimate, but nothing in it protects Morale. This account needs a separate body up front. See [galahad.md](galahad.md).

## Notes

Celestial, so he counts as any faction for the faction bonus, and adds +1% ATK and HP to the team just by being deployed. See [../factions.md](../factions.md).

Two 2026 guides call him the first Stargaze Station target, ahead of Elijah & Lailah, partly because he needs few copies to function. See [../recruitment.md](../recruitment.md).

## He is an Arena hero, and the guides do not say so

**He holds the running Arena attack comp** in [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md), and the account met him there first on the losing side. The guides read him as a long-fight hero because Morale climbs over time. **His opening is what Arena buys**, and both halves of it land in the first seconds.

- **The Morale opener gives Rolan and every hero of the most-deployed faction 20 Penetration and 50% less damage taken for 8s.** Eight seconds of halved damage is the length of the pre-digivolve window that has been killing Agumon.
- **The clone doubles the first Ultimate that matters.** It inherits 80% of the caster's base stats, so it copies **Supreme+ and an EX weapon at +10** when it copies Gaia Force.

**`unverified`: whether Celestial wildcards into the "most-deployed faction" clause.** Celestial counts as any faction for the **faction bonus**, and no source says whether the Morale clause reads it the same way. It decides whether the 8s covers Agumon or only Rolan and Aurora. **Watch Agumon's health bar drop rate in the opening 8 seconds.** See [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md).

**Arena caps hero levels and leaves skills alone, so his Epic tier is the whole of what he is missing.** Legendary+ is **3 Soul Sigils** away. See [../arena.md](../arena.md) and [../../account/progression.md](../../account/progression.md).
