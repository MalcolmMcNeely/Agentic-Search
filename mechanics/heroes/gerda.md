# Gerda

> **Covers:** Gerda kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

- **Faction:** Mauler
- **Class:** Tank
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 1
- **Level:** A

## Kit

Ultimate splashes healing water over an area, healing allies and putting enemies to sleep for up to 3s. Spring Therapy creates a zone healing 25% ally HP per second. Mighty Smash stuns and shields her.

## Best for

PvP and general play. A support tank.

## Notes

Weak in campaign and boss.
