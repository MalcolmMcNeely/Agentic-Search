# Solise

> **Covers:** Solise kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-11

- **Faction:** Wilder
- **Class:** Support
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 6
- **Rarity:** S

## Kit

**Ultimate, Life's Embrace.** Three healing waves sweep the field, and every allied **Bulbsprite** they touch fires a water ring from its host's tile that damages all enemies. So the heal is also the team's area damage.

**Lifebloom Buds.** She hands a **Bulb** to a non-summoned ally, healing it every 2s. Once that Bulb's total healing passes a threshold it **permanently becomes a Bulbsprite**, which raises the host's ATK by a share of Solise's own starting ATK.

**Floral Favor** is the third base skill.

**The Bulbsprite is the whole hero.** It is a summon, it is a permanent ally buff, and it is a damage source once the Ultimate lands. Everything else in her kit either creates one or reads one.

## Tier gates

**She stands at Mythic, so four of her six pieces are live and two are not.** See [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).

| Piece | Gate | Live at Mythic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Life's Embrace | none | yes |
| Lifebloom Buds | none | yes |
| Floral Favor | none | yes |
| Hero Focus | Legendary+ | yes |
| Resonant Bloom | Mythic+ | **no**, one step away |
| Enhance Force | Supreme+ | **no** |

**Hero Focus** adds **+10% ATK for the battle, and +5% more while an allied Bulbsprite is on the field**, rising to +14% and +11% at full skill level. Every heal and every Bulbsprite buff is a multiple of her ATK, so it compounds.

**Resonant Bloom is her Exclusive Equipment and it is one ascension step away.** She is **S-Level**, so Mythic to Mythic+ costs **2 Soul Sigils and 100 Faction Acorns**, the price the Digimon pair, Eironn, Aurora, Shemira and Galahad all paid. **That is the cheapest Exclusive Equipment left on the account.** See [../../account/equipment.md](../../account/equipment.md).

**Enhance Force needs Supreme+.** While Life's Embrace is casting, Bulbsprite damage adds extra damage worth 2% of the excess healing that Bulbsprite has stored.

**No shop stocks her.** The Dream Store shelf is A-Level only and its Wilder row is Arden, Lyca, Damian, Kafra and Parisa, so her copies come off a banner. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md).

**She holds slot 5 of the Epic wishlist, and that slot is the whole route.** She took it from Shakir the moment he was pulled: her next 2 sigils buy an Exclusive Equipment where his next four buy a Hero Focus. See [../recruitment.md](../recruitment.md).

## Best for

Campaign, boss and PvP. Healing that also scales team damage, so she pays into both sums at once by the reckoning in [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md).

**She stands in three fielded comps**: Arcane Labyrinth, Tower of Nature and Battle Drills Team 2. See [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md).

**She feeds Aurora.** Bulbsprites are summons, so Dream Veil raises their damage and Aurora's EX weapon at +5 raises it a further 8%. See [../team-archetypes.md](../team-archetypes.md).
