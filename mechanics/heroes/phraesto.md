# Phraesto

> **Covers:** Phraesto kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-01

- **Faction:** Hypogean
- **Class:** Tank
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 1
- **Rarity:** S

## Kit

**He deploys as two heroes.** In the preparation phase he spends 12% of his max HP to summon an **Illusion** that inherits **100% of his stats**, casts every skill he has, and acts on its own. Standing both in the same row gives each of them **30% damage reduction** until one dies.

| Skill | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Futile Echo, Ultimate | summons the Illusion at the start, then deals 200% damage within 2 tiles and heals him 160% HP per enemy hit |
| Crimson Contract | allies behind the **Illusion** take less damage; allies behind the **true body** gain Energy Recovery Speed |
| Vicious Sting | inflicts Paralyzing Venom, cutting enemy Haste and Vitality while draining their HP |
| Foggy Whisper | taunts and stuns the enemy's highest damage dealer, and resummons the Illusion if it falls |
| Enhance Force | shields both bodies while they share a row |
| Hero Focus, **Legendary+** | raises DEF on both bodies, and generates Energy whenever either takes damage |
| Exclusive Equipment, **Mythic+** | at +10, skill damage rises to 700% and both bodies open the fight with a shield worth 570% of ATK for 12s |

**Which body an ally stands behind decides which buff it gets.** Damage reduction sits behind the Illusion, Energy behind him. That makes his placement a real choice, not a default. See [../formation.md](../formation.md).

## Best for

He is a **stall tank**, so he wants a long fight: AFK Stages and Infinite Stages, Arcane Labyrinth, and Arena.

**The three current lists disagree about him in every mode, and the gaps are the widest on this account's roster.**

**Campaign** `unverified`: rated **S+** as PvE [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated], **S** in story mode [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated] and **A+** [afk.global Campaign tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated].

**Dream Realm** `unverified`, and this is a five-band split: **S** [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated], **A** [afk.global Dream Realm tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated] and **C** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. All three readings stand.

**Dream Realm Endless, rated A** [afk.global Dream Realm Endless tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated].

**PvP** `unverified`: **S+** [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated], **A** [afk.global PvP tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated] and **B** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated].

**He is the strongest Hypogean or Celestial this account can still add**, ranked third behind Elijah & Lailah and Rolan, both already owned. See [../recruitment.md](../recruitment.md).

## Tier gate

**He is Epic, so both of his gated skills are off, and every letter above rates a hero with them on.** Hero Focus needs **Legendary+**. Exclusive Equipment needs **Mythic+**, and that is the step that raises his skill damage to 700% and opens the fight with a 570% shield on both bodies. Five ascensions, costing seven Soul Sigils, separate the copy on the roster from that weapon.

None of the three lists states an investment level, so none of them says which Phraesto it is describing. allclash's own build-priority line treats him as a long project: it calls Hypogean heroes slow and hard to build, and ranks him second of the faction to build, behind the twins. Read the S+ as a ceiling, not as a reading of this account's copy. See [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md) and [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).

## Against this account

**He holds the AFK Stages push comp**, as its **only** Tank and one of its five Hands of Resonance. He heals himself off his own Ultimate, taunts the enemy's biggest hitter away from the carry, and hands out damage reduction from two bodies at once, so he pays into the survival sum four ways, which is what [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md) asks of a defensive slot. See [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md).

**Four ways is not enough on its own, and the account says so.** That comp is reported dead in the opening 10 seconds with only Agumon left standing, and holding an Infinite Stage front row alone on an **Epic** copy with two of his five pieces switched off is the reason. He is not the hero to drop; he is the hero to stand a second Tank beside. See [daimon.md](daimon.md) and [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md).

**Hypogean wildcards, so he costs no faction bonus.** He counts as any faction for the stat total and adds +1% ATK and HP just by deploying. He is the account's **first Hypogean**. See [../factions.md](../factions.md).

**His HP-based damage suits an uncapped ladder.** Guides call him weak against early bosses because his damage reads the enemy's health rather than his own ATK. That is the same argument [../../account/progression.md](../../account/progression.md) already uses for Starshard Spell: on Infinite Stages, where enemy defence climbs with no ceiling, damage that reads enemy HP is the kind that keeps working. It is also the likeliest reason the Dream Realm letters split so far, because a single armoured boss is the fight his kit reads worst.

**He is the buy directly after Elijah & Lailah.** Three Soul Sigils take him to Legendary+ and switch Hero Focus on. The twins' three sigils are about three months of Guild Medals away. Aurora is the only other hero this shelf can ascend and she is already **Mythic+**, so nothing on it competes with him now. See [../guild-store.md](../guild-store.md).

**He is barred from Legend Trial.** The four faction towers refuse Hypogean and Celestial heroes, and those towers hold this account's largest block of unclaimed rewards. He pays nothing there. See [../legend-trial.md](../legend-trial.md).

`unverified`: **whether the Illusion's Ultimates count toward Starshard Spell.** The artifact fires its true-damage wave every 3 **ally** Ultimates. The Illusion casts all his skills and acts as its own unit, so it may double his contribution to that counter. No source states it either way. Testing it is one battle: field him in the push comp and watch how often the wave lands. See [../artifacts.md](../artifacts.md).
