# Phraesto

> **Covers:** Phraesto kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-30

- **Faction:** Hypogean
- **Class:** Tank
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 1
- **Level:** S

## Kit

**He deploys as two heroes.** In the preparation phase he spends 12% of his max HP to summon an **Illusion** that inherits **100% of his stats**, casts every skill he has, and acts on its own. Standing both in the same row gives each of them **30% damage reduction** until one dies.

| Skill | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Futile Echo, Ultimate | summons the Illusion at the start, then deals 200% damage within 2 tiles and heals him 160% HP per enemy hit |
| Crimson Contract | allies behind the **Illusion** take less damage; allies behind the **true body** gain Energy Recovery Speed |
| Vicious Sting | inflicts Paralyzing Venom, cutting enemy Haste and Vitality while draining their HP |
| Foggy Whisper | taunts and stuns the enemy's highest damage dealer, and resummons the Illusion if it falls |
| Enhance Force | shields both bodies while they share a row |
| Hero Focus, **Legendary+** | raises DEF on both bodies, and generates Energy whenever either takes damage |
| Exclusive Equipment, **Mythic+** | at +10, skill damage rises to 700% and both bodies open the fight with a shield worth 570% of ATK for 12s |

**Which body an ally stands behind decides which buff it gets.** Damage reduction sits behind the Illusion, Energy behind him. That makes his placement a real choice, not a default. See [../formation.md](../formation.md).

## Best for

AFK Stages and Infinite Stages, Arcane Labyrinth, and Arena. He is a **stall tank**, so he wants a long fight. afk.global rates him Campaign A+, Boss A, PvP A.

**He is the strongest Hypogean or Celestial this account can still add**, ranked third behind Elijah & Lailah and Rolan, both already owned. See [../recruitment.md](../recruitment.md).

## Against this account

**He fills the hole the push comp names itself.** [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md) lists *no healer* as that comp's first weak point, and Daimon as its only body holding the front. Phraesto is a second Tank who heals himself off his own Ultimate, taunts the enemy's biggest hitter away from the carry, and hands out damage reduction. He pays into the survival sum twice over, which is what [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md) asks of a defensive slot.

**Hypogean wildcards, so he costs no faction bonus.** He counts as any faction for the stat total and adds +1% ATK and HP just by deploying. He is the account's **first Hypogean**. See [../factions.md](../factions.md).

**His HP-based damage suits an uncapped ladder.** Guides call him weak against early bosses because his damage reads the enemy's health rather than his own ATK. That is the same argument [../../account/progression.md](../../account/progression.md) already uses for Starshard Spell: on Infinite Stages, where enemy defence climbs with no ceiling, damage that reads enemy HP is the kind that keeps working.

`unverified`: **whether the Illusion's Ultimates count toward Starshard Spell.** The artifact fires its true-damage wave every 3 **ally** Ultimates. The Illusion casts all his skills and acts as its own unit, so it may double his contribution to that counter. No source states it either way. Testing it is one battle: field him in the push comp and watch how often the wave lands. See [../artifacts.md](../artifacts.md).

**He is at Epic, so Hero Focus is off.** Three Soul Sigils take him to Legendary+ and switch it on. The Guild Store stocks him, but its medals are booked on Elijah & Lailah for about three months, then Aurora. See [../guild-store.md](../guild-store.md).

**He is barred from Legend Trial.** The four faction towers refuse Hypogean and Celestial heroes, and those towers hold this account's largest block of unclaimed rewards. He pays nothing there. See [../legend-trial.md](../legend-trial.md).
