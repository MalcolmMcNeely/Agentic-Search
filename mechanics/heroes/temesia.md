## Kit

Ultimate Knight's Heart charges the farthest enemy, damaging everything in the path and restoring Energy on impact. She can summon her mount to leap forward and knock down adjacent foes.

## Best for

A durable frontline with interrupts and knockdowns. Controls enemy positioning.
