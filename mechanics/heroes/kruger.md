# Kruger

> **Covers:** Kruger kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-30

- **Faction:** Mauler
- **Class:** Warrior
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 1
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

Ultimate Devastating Axe knocks an enemy down for 1.5s for 450% damage. Stacks Shatter Armor to strip enemy defence, then triggers Vital Strike on weakened targets for heavy healing.

## Hero Focus

Unlocks at **Legendary+**, which this account holds. See [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md).

**Increases Ranged DEF during battle**: 16 at level 1, 21 at level 2, 26 at level 3. Every point of Ranged DEF cuts damage taken from **non-adjacent** enemies by 1%, so level 1 is a 16% cut against ranged attackers and nothing at all against the melee standing next to him.

It is a defensive buff, so it buys time alive and no damage. See [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md).

`?` Which of the three levels this account holds. Hero Focus levels are bought with **Tidal Essence**, not earned by ascending, so the tier alone does not settle it.

## Exclusive Equipment

Unlocks at **Mythic+**, three ascension steps above this account. See [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md).

At battle start he gains a shield worth **40% of his HP for 15s, and control immunity, only while no ally stands within 1 tile**. He also gains 30 Life Drain under the same isolation condition. Upgrades raise the shield to 44% and 48%, and one removes the distance limit on Vital Strike.

**The isolation clause makes it worthless in his only comp.** The Tower of Will comp in [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md) puts Antandra, Kordan and Kruger all at range 1, so an ally is always within 1 tile and the shield never fires. His copies are stocked in the Dream Store, and 22 sigils buy nothing here. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md).

## Best for

Boss fights and one-on-one.

## Notes

Weak in campaign and PvP because he is single-target only.

Hero Focus does not change that. Ranged DEF answers an enemy backline, and his problem is that he hits one target at a time.
