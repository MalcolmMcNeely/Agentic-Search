# Aurora

> **Covers:** Aurora kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

- **Faction:** Celestial
- **Class:** Mage
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 2, or 3 with Enhance Force
- **Level:** S

## Kit

Ultimate Starlit Slumber summons Sonny the unicorn, who inherits a share of her HP. She summons magical bunnies while asleep and turns nearby enemies into immobilised plushies. Dream Veil buffs every allied summon's damage, protects melee allies and strengthens ranged summon attacks.

## Best for

Campaign and boss, rated A+. Best on teams stacked with summons.

## Notes

Celestial, so she counts as any faction for the faction bonus.
