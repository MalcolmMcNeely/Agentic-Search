# Aurora

> **Covers:** Aurora kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-30

- **Faction:** Celestial
- **Class:** Mage
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 2, or 3 with Enhance Force
- **Level:** S

## Kit

Ultimate Starlit Slumber summons Sonny the unicorn, who inherits a share of her HP. She summons magical bunnies while asleep and turns nearby enemies into immobilised plushies. Dream Veil buffs every allied summon's damage, protects melee allies and strengthens ranged summon attacks.

## Hero Focus

Unlocks at **Legendary+**, and this account holds Mythic, so it is live. See [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md).

**Aurora gains +10% ATK during battle, and a further +1% ATK per allied summon belonging to a different ally, up to 5 stacks.** Level 2 raises that to +13% and +1.5% per summon; level 3 to +16% and +2%.

**This is a second summon-stack payoff, pointed at Aurora herself.** Dream Veil sends her buff out to the team's summons. Hero Focus brings the team's summons back to her as ATK. So the more allies that field summons, the more both halves pay, and she is the only hero on the roster whose own damage scales off other heroes' summons. See [../team-archetypes.md](../team-archetypes.md).

The cap counts summons from **different** allies, so five summons from one hero do not fill it. Spread the summon sources across the comp.

`?` Which of the three levels this account holds. Hero Focus levels are bought with **Tidal Essence**, not earned by ascending, so the tier alone does not settle it.

## Best for

Campaign and boss, rated A+. Best on teams stacked with summons.

## Notes

Celestial, so she counts as any faction for the faction bonus.

**Sonny is a liability as well as a payload.** Aurora is Invincible while she sleeps, but if Sonny dies she is jolted awake, loses that invincibility, and can cast nothing but her Ultimate. Protecting Sonny is protecting Aurora.
