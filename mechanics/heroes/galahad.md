> **Covers:** Galahad kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

- **Faction:** Mauler
- **Class:** Mage
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 10
- **Level:** S

## Kit

Ultimate Temporal Field damages all enemies then draws a Magic Circle centred on her. Once the circle first completes, Magister Merlin's skills become 20% stronger and last 100% longer. At Ex rarity every Merlin skill cast spawns a shadow that repeats it for 60% of the HP loss.

## Best for

Campaign and boss. Top-tier magic damage.
