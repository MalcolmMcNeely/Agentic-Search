# Korin

> **Covers:** Korin kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-09

- **Faction:** Lightbearer
- **Class:** Warrior
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 2
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

| Skill | Gate | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demonseal Spear, Ultimate | none | sweeps within **2 tiles** for **70% damage 4 times** and **immobilises 2s**, knocking adjacent enemies back 1 tile |
| Defiance Charge | none | jumps beside the **weakest ally** and shields them both against **300% damage for 5s**, then dashes to the closest enemy for **180% true damage** |
| Air Strike | none | attacks against enemies 1 tile beyond are **guaranteed crits**, and the crit damage rises with his Crit Rate |
| Hero Focus | **Legendary+** | **+12 Haste** for the whole battle |
| Vine Arms, Exclusive Equipment | **Mythic+** | after **3 Ultimate casts by Korin and nearby allies**, his spear adds **true damage worth a percentage of the target's max HP for 8s** |
| Enhance Force | **Supreme+** | **25% less damage** taken from distant enemies |

**Both untiered skills level with the hero, and this account is past every step.** The roster fields at **265**, so Demonseal Spear immobilises for **4s** and hits for **85%**, and Defiance Charge shields for **320%** and dashes for **220%**. See [../../account/progression.md](../../account/progression.md).

`unverified`: **the shield skill's name.** afk.global calls it **Defiance Charge**; prydwen calls it **All-round Tactic**. Both describe the same effect with the same numbers, so only the label is in doubt.

## Tier gate

**He is Epic, so three of his six pieces are live and three are dark.** The Ultimate, Defiance Charge and Air Strike all work. Hero Focus, Vine Arms and Enhance Force do not. See [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).

**He is A-Level and he is on the Dream Store shelf**, so his copies cost Dream Fragments rather than banner sigils. Epic to Legendary+ is **17 Soul Sigils** and switches Hero Focus on. Mythic+, which opens Vine Arms, is **39** from here. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md).

## Best for

**No current vetted tier list ranks him in any mode.** allclash does not name him, and afk.global places him only on its blended **Overall** tab, which fails the three-part rule and is not citable as a letter. That is guide silence, so his place comes from the kit. See [../tier-list-sources.md](../tier-list-sources.md) and [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md).

**Time alive.** A **4s immobilise on everything within 2 tiles** is the largest area control any Warrior on this account brings, and an enemy that cannot act deals nothing. The knockback resets a melee push. Defiance Charge then shields him and the **weakest** ally, which is the ally the enemy is already killing, and he picks that target himself rather than by placement.

**Damage out.** The dash is **220% true damage**, so it ignores enemy defence. Air Strike turns his reach advantage into guaranteed crits. The Ultimate adds four area hits on top.

**So he is a control Warrior with a true damage tap, and he is a mitigation slot second.** See [../team-archetypes.md](../team-archetypes.md).

**Range 2 is what places him.** He is a Warrior, so the default row is the front, but he reaches a tile further than a range-1 melee and Air Strike pays him for hitting the enemy 1 tile beyond. Stand him where that second tile is enemies rather than wall. He also jumps out of position on Defiance Charge, so nothing may be built on him holding a tile. See [../formation.md](../formation.md).

**He is fielded in no comp.** Every comp in [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md) already holds a higher-tier hero in his lane.
