# Thador

> **Covers:** Thador kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-07

- **Faction:** Wilder
- **Class:** Tank
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 1
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

Ultimate draws a ritual zone that cuts enemy Crit DMG DEF and sets off shadow eruptions that heal the team. Protects a designated lieutenant ally and grinds out damage with Darkmoon.

**The lieutenant is chosen by placement, in the preparation phase.** Thador designates **the ally standing 1 tile behind him**, and shields himself and that ally for **350% + 35% damage over 8s**, rising to 360% at level 71, 370% at level 131 and **380% + 35% at level 191**. So the tile behind Thador is a decision, not a leftover. See [../formation.md](../formation.md).

## Tier gates

**He stands at Mythic, so his Hero Focus is live and his Exclusive Equipment is one step away.** See [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).

**Markmoon Pact is the Exclusive Equipment and needs Mythic+.** It raises the lieutenant's Crit Rate, and **at EX +10 it also hands that ally 350 Energy at battle start**. So the Energy is two gates deep: the weapon slot opens at Mythic+ and the Energy step needs ten levels on top of that. **No comp may credit Thador with battle-start Energy today.**

Mythic to Mythic+ is **12 Soul Sigils** for an A-Level hero. The Dream Store does not stock him, so those copies come off a banner. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md).

## Best for

Campaign and PvP. A tanky frontline with support built in.

**He is the front row of four fielded comps**: Tower of Nature, Arcane Labyrinth, Battle Drills Team 2 and the Ravaged Realm Sylvan Squad. At Mythic he is now the joint-highest tier in every one of them. See [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md).
