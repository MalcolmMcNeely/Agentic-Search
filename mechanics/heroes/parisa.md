# Parisa

> **Covers:** Parisa kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-11

- **Faction:** Wilder
- **Class:** Mage
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 5
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

**Floral Splendor**, her Ultimate, marks an unmarked enemy with a flower that lasts the rest of the battle, then deals **480% damage to every flower-marked enemy and anything within 1 tile of them**. Each cast adds a flower and hits all of them, so the area she covers grows through the fight rather than resetting.

**Floral Inspiration** gives **herself and the 1 nearest ally +22 ATK SPD and +22% normal attack damage for 10s**, on a 10s cooldown. That is the one effect on the roster that raises **normal attack damage** rather than skill damage.

**Flower Power** fires a bouquet after every **3 normal attacks** that expands toward her target and deals **200% damage to everything along the path**.

`disputed`: **the name of the buff skill.** afk.global calls it Floral Inspiration; prydwen and the fan guides call it Wilder Blessing. The effect is the same in both, so the numbers stand and only the label is open.

## Her tile picks who she buffs, and it picks the flower

Floral Inspiration reads the **1 nearest ally**, so the hero standing beside her takes the whole buff and everyone else takes nothing. Flower Power fires **along a path** to her target, so her row decides what the bouquet passes through. See [../formation.md](../formation.md).

At range **5** she stands mid-grid, not back, which is where the ally she is buffing has to be as well.

## Tier gate

**She is Epic, and all three ascension pieces are dark.**

| Piece | Unlocks at | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hero Focus** | Legendary+ | +12% ATK during battle |
| **Falling Blossom**, the Exclusive Equipment | Mythic+ | marks the enemy in the **symmetrical position** to her own tile with a flower at battle start, so Floral Splendor's first cast already has a target |
| **Enhance Force** | Supreme+ | Flower Power needs **1 fewer** normal attack to fire |

She is **A-Level**, so Mythic+ costs **12 Soul Sigils**. **The Dream Store stocks her**, so her copies come off the Wilder shelf for Dream Fragments rather than a banner. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md) and [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md).

**afk.global labels every one of her skills a base skill, and that is wrong.** Its own page lists Hero Focus, Falling Blossom and Enhance Force with no gate, and those three are the standard Legendary+, Mythic+ and Supreme+ unlocks. Take the gates from [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md), not from that page.

## Best for

**Buff converter, engine half.** She hands a neighbour temporary ATK SPD and normal attack damage, which is the resource Silven banks into permanent stats and Lily May grows her ATK from. See [../team-archetypes.md](../team-archetypes.md).

Her own damage is a slow area ramp: every Ultimate adds a flower and every flower is hit again by the next one, so she pays in a long fight and not in an Arena race. By the reckoning in [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md) she pays into the damage sum only, with nothing for survival.

**No current tier list on the vetted list rates her**, so she carries no letter. Her place here comes from her kit alone. See [../tier-list-sources.md](../tier-list-sources.md).
