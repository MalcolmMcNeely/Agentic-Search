# Silven

> **Covers:** Silven kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-01

- **Faction:** Lightbearer
- **Class:** Marksman
- **Damage:** Physical, with a true damage component
- **Range:** ranged, exact tiles not published
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

Ultimate Aloft Edge summons 6 flying blades that launch with each normal attack for 8s, hitting harder per blade. Normal attacks build Blademarks into Gravity Collapse with a knockdown. Oath of Fealty turns ally buffs into Energy and permanent stats.

## Best for

He is a **ramp carry**. Aloft Edge pays per normal attack, Blademarks build over hits, and Oath of Fealty converts every ally buff he receives into Energy and permanent stats, so his damage climbs the longer the fight runs and the more buffs land on him. Bring allies that hand out temporary buffs, and expect little from him in the opening seconds. See [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md) and [../team-archetypes.md](../team-archetypes.md).

**No two current lists agree on him in any mode, and one of them rates him a skip.**

**Campaign** `unverified`, a five-band split: rated **S** [afk.global Campaign tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated], **A** in story mode [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated] and **B+** as PvE [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated]. All three readings stand.

**Dream Realm** `unverified`: **A+** [afk.global Dream Realm tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated], **A** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated] and **B+** [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated]. allclash gives its reason: it reads him as working less well in Dream Realm than Shemira, and this account holds Shemira at **Supreme with an EX weapon** against his Elite+, so the comparison lands even harder here than in the guide.

**Dream Realm Endless, rated S** [afk.global Dream Realm Endless tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated]. His true damage component is the reason to take that seriously, because Endless pays for damage that ignores defence. afk.global is also the only current list that separates Endless from blended Dream Realm. See [../game-modes.md](../game-modes.md).

**PvP** `unverified`: **A+** [afk.global PvP tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated], **A** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated] and **B+** [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated]. allclash calls PvP the one mode where it sees promise in him, on his fast Energy activation, while still rating him B+.

## Tier gate

**He is Elite+, the second-lowest ascension tier in the game, and no list is describing that hero.** **Hero Focus needs Legendary+, four steps up. Exclusive Equipment needs Mythic+, six steps up.** None of the three lists states an investment level, so all three letters are ceilings for a copy this account is nowhere near.

He is **A-Level**, so his copies are the cheap kind: the Dream Store stocks A-Level heroes, which makes ascending him a matter of Dream Fragments rather than banner luck. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md), [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md) and [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).

Wants allies that hand out temporary buffs, because Oath of Fealty turns each one into Energy and permanent stats.
