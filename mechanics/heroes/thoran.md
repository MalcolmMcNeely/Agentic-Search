# Thoran

> **Covers:** Thoran kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-03

- **Faction:** Graveborn
- **Class:** Tank
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 1
- **Rarity:** S

## Kit

**Ultimate, Soul Retaliation.** He charges for a moment, then slashes every enemy in range for **200% damage plus 180% of the damage he took during the charge**, and gains **20% Life Drain**. He cannot be interrupted while casting, so the enemy cannot answer the charge with control.

**Skill I, Soul Plunder.** He picks the enemy with the **highest HP** that he has not plundered yet, drains **18% of its current HP** and adds that to his own **max HP**, and makes that enemy take **13% more damage for the rest of the battle**.

**Skill II, Resurrection.** He revives with **40% HP**, once per battle.

**Hero Focus, Legendary+.** Normal attacks and skills give him **+24% Energy Recovery**, and **+6% more while Resurrection is still untriggered**.

**Exclusive Equipment, Soul Pact, Mythic+.** Before the fight he pacts with the **ally standing behind him**: he absorbs **50% of that ally's damage**, **25% of the damage that ally deals heals him**, and if he falls the ally spends **85% of their own HP** to heal him for **150%** of it.

## Best for

The Graveborn frontline, and **Tower of Eternity** above all, because that tower is faction-locked and Graveborn is the account's thinnest S-Level faction. See [../legend-trial.md](../legend-trial.md).

He pays into both sums at once, by the reckoning in [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md). Resurrection and the stolen max HP are survival. Soul Plunder's permanent **13% damage amplify** on the biggest enemy is offence the whole team shares, so he is a **boss amplify** hero wearing a Tank label. His Ultimate turns damage taken into damage dealt, which is the same trade Koko makes. See [../team-archetypes.md](../team-archetypes.md).

**Legendary+ is the cheapest upgrade on him and the next one to buy.** An S-Level hero crosses Legendary to Legendary+ for **2 Soul Sigils**, and that switches Hero Focus on. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md).

**Soul Pact is his Exclusive Equipment, not a base skill, and that changes who he is at every tier below Mythic+.** playafkjourney lists it flat, as a fourth skill with no gate. That page is not a second reading: it also states his **Rarity as "Mythic"**, which is an ascension tier and not a rarity at all, so it conflates the two scales and cannot be trusted on a tier gate. talkandroid names Soul Pact the Exclusive Weapon, and every Exclusive Equipment in the game unlocks at Mythic+. See [../equipment-and-charms.md](../equipment-and-charms.md) and [README.md](README.md).
