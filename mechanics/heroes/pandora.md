# Pandora

> **Covers:** Pandora kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

- **Faction:** Dimensional
- **Class:** Support
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 5
- **Rarity:** S

## Kit

Ultimate Panic Projection opens a box that makes all units flee for 5s while losing heavy HP, then cuts enemy ATK. Boxed Blessing seals an ally in an invincible box for 4s, then releases them with bonus Energy and ATK. Applies Corruption debuffs.

## Best for

Boss and PvP. Protects one key ally while debuffing the enemy.
