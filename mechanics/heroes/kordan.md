# Kordan

> **Covers:** Kordan kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-01

- **Faction:** Mauler
- **Class:** Warrior
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 1
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

Ultimate Dominance Ring summons a hunting circle that immobilises the target for 5s and buffs allies standing inside. Fury Slash deals damage plus a shield, and Sundering Strike knocks down. Bonuses scale with how much HP the enemy has lost.

## Hero Focus

Unlocks at **Legendary+**, which this account holds. See [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md).

**Increases his own ATK during battle**: 12% at level 1, 16% at level 2, 20% at level 3.

It is a flat personal damage buff with no clause on it, so it pays in every fight he is deployed in and never in the ally half of his kit. His Dominance Ring still buffs the allies standing inside; Hero Focus does not feed it. See [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md).

`?` Which of the three levels this account holds. Hero Focus levels are bought with **Tidal Essence**, not earned by ascending, so the tier alone does not settle it.

## Exclusive Equipment

Unlocks at **Mythic+**, two ascension steps above this account. See [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md).

## Best for

Campaign, boss and PvP. A melee buffer and tanky initiator.

## Notes

afk.global's page lists faction and class as Unknown. The hero launch video and other sources say Mauler Warrior. Falls off hard in Dream Realm Endless.

allclash rates one copy and no ascension as enough for anyone not playing PvP [2026-01-14]. The account is past that, so the Hero Focus is a bonus over the recommended stopping point rather than a target reached.
