# Kordan

> **Covers:** Kordan kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-10

- **Faction:** Mauler
- **Class:** Warrior
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 1
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

| Skill | Gate | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dominance Ring, Ultimate | none | opens at **1000 Energy**. Immobilises the target **5s**, hits everything in the circle for **180%**, and the circle stands **16s**. **Every unit inside takes 60% less damage and 60% less healing from outside it.** Allies inside gain ATK, damage and Life Drain |
| Fury Slash | level 11 | **460% + 40%** damage, and shields him for **100% of the damage dealt plus 300%**, lasting **15s**. 8s cooldown |
| Sundering Strike | level 31 | **500% + 50%** damage and a knockdown. 10s cooldown |
| Hero Focus | **Legendary+** | **+12% ATK** at level 1, **16%** at 2, **20%** at 3, for the whole battle |
| Rage Unleashed, Exclusive Equipment | **Mythic+** | upgrades the two skills above. See below |
| Enhance Force | **Supreme+** | the circle **leaps to a new target** and moves with it, lasts **10s longer**, and allies inside gain **50 Penetration** |

**Every level threshold in his kit is behind this account.** The roster fields at **270**, and the last one is 231. So Dominance Ring already hands allies inside the circle **36% + 3% ATK**, **36% + 3% damage** and **60 Life Drain**, and Fury Slash and Sundering Strike are past their level 191 and 211 steps. **His ally buff is at its ceiling and no ascension raises it.** See [../../account/progression.md](../../account/progression.md).

`?` which of the three Hero Focus levels the account holds. Those levels are bought with **Tidal Essence**, not earned by ascending, so the tier does not settle it.

## Rage Unleashed

**It is the Mythic+ skill slot itself, not a passive bolted onto the kit.** It arms on his first assist or kill of a non-summoned enemy inside the circle, or after **20s** of fighting inside it. Then both basic skills change:

- **Fury Slash** adds damage worth **30% of the target's lost HP**, capped at **600% of his ATK**.
- **Sundering Strike** knocks the target **into the air**, adds **2s** to the knockdown and **300% ATK**.

| Level | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| +5 | Fury Slash to **35% of lost HP, cap 700%**; Sundering Strike to **350%** |
| +10 | each assist or kill inside the circle permanently grants **+40 Phys DEF and Magic DEF, stacking 3 times**, and restores **15% of max HP per second for 3s** |
| +15 | Fury Slash to **40% of lost HP, cap 800%**; Sundering Strike to **400%** |

`unverified`: **what +5 and +10 each contain.** The wiki's in-game tooltip text gives the table above. lootbar [2026-03-20] instead puts the DEF stack at **+5** and states it as **+40%** rather than +40 flat, with the heal at +10. Both readings stand.

**Refine 2 and Refine 4 pay only in Rivalry Mode**: 10% less damage taken for 20s and then 10% more damage dealt for 20s, plus 15 Life Drain and 10 Execution. `?` no source gives a Refine 1 or Refine 3 effect.

## Tier gate

**He is Mythic, so four of his six pieces are live and two are dark.** Dominance Ring, Fury Slash, Sundering Strike and Hero Focus all work. **Rage Unleashed and Enhance Force do not.**

**Mythic itself unlocked nothing.** The unlock column reads obtain, level 11, level 31, Legendary+, Mythic+, Supreme+, and no skill sits on Mythic. The step bought stats and a Magic Charm quality bump, and his Hero Focus was already live before it. See [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md).

**Mythic to Mythic+ is 12 Soul Sigils, and the Dream Store does not stock him.** So his copies have no Dream Fragment route and must come off the All-Hero banner. That makes him the worst-supplied of the eight heroes sitting one step from an Exclusive Equipment. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md) and [../../account/equipment.md](../../account/equipment.md).

## Best for

**Two vetted sources rank him, and they agree except on PvP.**

| Source | PvE / Story | Dream Realm | PvP | Investment stated |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [allclash](https://www.allclash.com/best-heroes-tier-list-in-afk-2-journey/) [2026-09-08] | B | A | **A+** | Yes, by name: "outside of PvP you really only need one copy of him. No EX Weapon, no Hero Focus" |
| [Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/afk-journey/tier-list) [2026-08-17] | B | A | **B** | No |

`unverified`: **PvP, A+ against B.** Both sit inside the window and neither is the weaker source. Dream Realm agrees at A.

**allclash's investment line is the useful half, and this account is well past it.** It says one copy and no ascension is enough outside PvP. The account holds Mythic with Hero Focus live, so **every tier bought so far was bought for PvP and for the weapon at the end of it**, not for the fights he is actually fielded in.

**He is an initiator and a zone, not a damage dealer.** The Ultimate opens at 1000 Energy, so it lands early, immobilises for 5s and then plants a 16s circle that buffs whoever stands in it. Fury Slash shields him for the damage it deals, so he holds his own tile without a healer.

**The 60% clause cuts both ways, and it is the thing to build around.** Every unit inside the circle takes **60% less damage and 60% less healing from outside it**. So a ranged carry firing in from outside is firing at 40%, and a healer outside is healing at 40%. The circle is a room the comp has to walk into, not a buff it receives at range.

`unverified`: whether that clause reads the *source's* position or the *target's*. One reading, from the skill text. **It changes three fielded comps if it holds**, so settle it in one battle before building on it. See [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md).

**So he is a control and mitigation body that pays a zone buff**, and he sits under boss amplify only once Rage Unleashed is on. See [../team-archetypes.md](../team-archetypes.md).

`unverified`: **that he falls off hard in Dream Realm Endless.** The claim is carried from an earlier read of afk.global with no line to point at, and **both current sources rank him A for Dream Realm**, which is the opposite reading. Endless is a different fight from ranked Dream Realm, so the two can both be true, but nothing in window supports the Endless half.

**Range 1 places him in the front row**, and the circle lands on the target, so it pulls the rest of the comp forward with it. See [../formation.md](../formation.md).

**He is fielded in three comps:** Tower of Will, Battle Drills Team 3 and the Ravaged Realm Mauler squad. See [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md).
