# Antandra

> **Covers:** Antandra kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

- **Faction:** Mauler
- **Class:** Tank
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 1
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

Ultimate Shield Assault taunts, cuts damage taken by 60%, stuns adjacent foes for 2s, deals area damage and heals her. Shield Formation shields her and one ally. Gale Barrier adds damage reduction when the guarded ally drops below 50% HP.

## Best for

Campaign and PvP. A guard-style tank.

## Notes

Sources disagree on her class. afk.global's character page says Ranger, its own tier list says Marksman, ONE Esports says Tank. Her kit is taunt, shields and guarding, which is Tank behaviour, so Tank is recorded.
