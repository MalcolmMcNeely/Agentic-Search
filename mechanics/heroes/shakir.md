# Shakir

> **Covers:** Shakir kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-07

- **Faction:** Mauler
- **Class:** Rogue
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 1
- **Rarity:** S

## Kit

**Ultimate Ravaging Claws** leaps onto a tile, knocks up the enemy standing there and deals **190%** damage within 2 tiles. He is unaffected during the leap, and lands in **Wolf Form**.

**Wolf Form is the whole hero, and it burns Energy to stay on.** He spends **60 Energy a second** to hold it. While it runs his basic attack changes from one target to a frontal arc, and he projects **Lupine Aura** over 2 tiles: the arc damages every enemy inside it, and every ally inside it takes **20% less damage and gains 10 Haste**.

**Agonizing Rush** hits three times with rising damage, and hits the same frontal arc while he is in Wolf Form.

**Wolf's Will** is a passive giving Ranged DEF, plus ATK scaling off nearby allies. In Wolf Form he is unaffected and gains **30 Life Drain**.

So one slot pays into both sums at once: he mitigates for the allies standing near him and deals area damage himself. See [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md).

## Hero Focus

Unlocks at **Legendary+**, **three ascension steps above this account**, which stands at Epic. See [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md).

Constant damage reduction on himself, raised further while at least **2 non-summoned allies** stand under Lupine Aura. The bonus half reads the formation, so it pays only in a comp that bunches around him.

## Exclusive Equipment

**All-Conquering**, unlocks at **Mythic+**, four steps above this account.

Lets the Ultimate cast at **800 Energy**, extends Wolf Form and removes the 60-per-second Energy drain for 5s. That drain is his one hard limit, so this is the piece that turns a short Wolf Form into a long one.

## Enhance Force

Unlocks at **Supreme+**. The third hit of Agonizing Rush cuts the target's Vitality by 60 for 4s.

## Best for

Any Mauler comp. He is the faction's damage-reduction aura and a second area damage dealer in one slot, which is why the letters sit level across all three modes rather than spiking in one.

**Tower of Will is the fight he is aimed at**, at 53 of 90 and Mauler-locked. See [../legend-trial.md](../legend-trial.md) and [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md).

## Notes

**Range 1 puts him in the front row**, and Lupine Aura is a 2-tile bubble around him, so his placement decides who gets the 20% cut. Stand the fragile carry inside it. See [../formation.md](../formation.md).

**At Epic all three gated pieces are dark**: Hero Focus, All-Conquering and Enhance Force. The base Lupine Aura works now; the Energy-drain fix that makes Wolf Form last does not.

**He no longer holds an Epic wishlist slot.** Solise took slot 5 the moment he was pulled, because her next 2 Soul Sigils buy an Exclusive Equipment and his next four buy only a Hero Focus. He is the first name back in when she reaches Mythic+, and no shop stocks an S-Level hero, so until then he has no route to a copy. See [../recruitment.md](../recruitment.md).
