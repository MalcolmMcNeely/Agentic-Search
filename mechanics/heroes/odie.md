# Odie

> **Covers:** Odie kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-01

- **Faction:** Mauler
- **Class:** Marksman
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 5, and 7 from level 91
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

Ultimate Corrosive Dart deals 150% damage and applies **Dart Poison**, which then deals **30% damage a second until the target dies** and **cannot be dispelled**. Triple Tap hits one enemy 3 times for 60% each, and those hits count as normal attacks. Venom Surge points his normal attacks at the nearest poisoned target, and every hit on a poisoned target **permanently raises that target's Dart Poison damage by 25%, up to 20 stacks**.

So the poison is the damage and the normal attacks are the multiplier. Twenty stacks turn one dart into six times the tick it started at, and none of it can be cleansed off.

## Best for

**A scaling carry.** The damage is a stack count that only goes up, so he wants a long fight and four heroes keeping him alive and firing. See [../team-archetypes.md](../team-archetypes.md).

**PvE A+, Dream Realm A, PvP A** [allclash, 2026-08-26]. That source states no single investment level, but it says plainly that **he needs Mythic+ or higher to do a great job**, so read all three letters as describing a hero four ascensions above where this account holds him.

He is Magic damage on a Marksman, which is rare. Every other Marksman on this roster except Bonnie is Physical, so he answers a fight that resists Physical without giving up the Marksman range.

## Tier gate

**The account holds him at Elite, and every one of his three ascension skills is off.** See [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md) and [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).

| Skill | Unlocks at | Live | What is missing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero Focus | Legendary+ | no | +21 ATK SPD, up to 33. It is the stack engine: faster normal attacks stack Dart Poison faster |
| Heart Crusher, the Exclusive Equipment skill | Mythic+ | no | instantly kills a poisoned enemy under an HP threshold, and the threshold rises after each kill |
| Enhance Force | Supreme+ | no | Triple Tap hits poisoned targets harder |

**Elite Odie is the base kit only, and the base kit is the slowest half of it.** Hero Focus is the first target, because attack speed is what feeds the 20 stacks. Heart Crusher is the skill the guides mean when they call him the best A-Level hero, and it is four ascensions away.

**His copies come from the Dream Store if the shelf stocks him.** He is A-Level, which is the only rarity that shelf holds. Check [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md) before planning around it, because the shelf on this account is a fixed subset and several A-Level heroes are absent from it.
