# Eironn

> **Covers:** Eironn kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-31

- **Faction:** Wilder
- **Class:** Rogue
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 1
- **Rarity:** S

## Kit

Ultimate Verdant Cyclone marks a tile, drags every enemy within 2 tiles to the centre, damages them and immobilises them for 3s. Ice Spike hits a 3-tile arc, cutting Haste, then thrusts 2 tiles to hit the same targets and anything behind them, cutting Magic DEF by 40%. Tempest Guard gives him a shield blocking 280% damage for 10s and 50% Dodge Rate while it holds. Howling Hurricane lets the Ultimate fire once per battle with no Energy. Enhance Force deepens the Magic DEF cut on immobilised targets.

## Best for

He is an **engine, not a carry**: the pull bunches the enemy so an AoE ally can delete the stack. See [../team-archetypes.md](../team-archetypes.md).

**Campaign** `unverified`: rated **S** as PvE [allclash, 2026-08-26, assumes the EX weapon] and **S** [afk.global Campaign tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated], against **A** in story mode [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated].

**PvP, rated A+** [allclash, 2026-08-26, assumes the EX weapon] and **A+** [afk.global PvP tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated], with **A** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. The three land in the same letter, which makes PvP his best-evidenced mode.

**Dream Realm is where his kit stops paying, and all three lists say so** `unverified` only on the last band: **C+** [allclash, 2026-08-26, assumes the EX weapon], **C** [afk.global Dream Realm tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated] and **D** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. A Dream Realm boss is one enemy, so a pull that bunches a crowd has nothing to bunch.

**Dream Realm Endless, rated C** [afk.global Dream Realm Endless tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated], for the same reason.

allclash reads him as an early-game engine rather than a lasting meta pick: strong for pushing the first weeks, less relevant later.

## Tier gate

**allclash names the Exclusive Equipment as the thing that turns him on**, saying he reads badly before it is built and becomes strong once it is. **Exclusive Equipment unlocks at Mythic+, and this account holds him at Mythic**, one ascension short. Hero Focus, at **Legendary+**, is live. See [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md) and [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).

## Notes

`disputed` on class. afk.global lists him as **Ranger**, a class that does not exist in the six-class list in [../equipment-and-charms.md](../equipment-and-charms.md). The Fandom wiki lists him as **Rogue**. Written as Rogue.

afk.global also shows his rarity as "Elite", which is an ascension tier, not a rarity. Every other source has him as S-Level, and he is selectable on the Epic Recruitment wishlist, which only takes S-Level heroes.
