> **Covers:** Bonnie kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

- **Faction:** Graveborn
- **Class:** Marksman
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 7
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

Ultimate fires magic arrows at all enemies, hitting harder and stunning any target carrying her Aging debuff. Core skills apply Aging to cut enemy Haste and ATK, then spread it to nearby foes.

## Best for

Campaign and PvP. Controls the enemy backline.
