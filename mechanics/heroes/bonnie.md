# Bonnie

> **Covers:** Bonnie kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-09

- **Faction:** Graveborn
- **Class:** Marksman
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 7
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

**Ultimate.** She fires magic arrows at every enemy, hitting harder against any target carrying **Aging** and stunning it.

**Aging** is the whole kit. Her core skills apply it to cut enemy **Haste and ATK**, then spread it to nearby foes. Cutting a boss's Haste slows every cast it makes, which [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md) counts as survival, not damage.

## Tier gates

**She stands at Supreme, so five of her six pieces are live and one is not.** That is the joint-highest tier on the account outside the Digimon pair, level with Eironn, Shemira and Smokey & Meerky. **She is the only A-Level hero on the account to reach it.** See [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).

| Piece | Gate | Live at Supreme |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ultimate | none | yes |
| Aging | none | yes |
| the third base skill | none | yes |
| Hero Focus | Legendary+ | yes |
| Blight Surge | Mythic+ | **yes** |
| Enhance Force | Supreme+ | **no** |

**Blight Surge is her Exclusive Equipment and it is now unlocked.** allclash names **EX +10** the point that matters for story and campaign play, and **+15** as a PvP-only extra that most accounts do not need. +15 needs Supreme+, so **+10 is her ceiling at Supreme**.

**The EX weapon stands at +1, so Blight Surge is open and barely spent.** Nine levels can be bought before the Supreme ceiling at +10: **150 Tidal Essence** each up to +5, then **75 Temporal Essence** each. That is **600 Tidal and 375 Temporal**, which ties Shemira and Faramor exactly, so none of the three is the cheap one any more. See [../../account/equipment.md](../../account/equipment.md).

**`?` how she got here.** She is **A-Level and the Dream Store does not stock her**, so every copy from Mythic to Supreme came off a banner. That is a large number of duplicates for a hero no shelf sells, and it bears on the open rarity question in [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md).

## Best for

Campaign and PvP. She controls the enemy backline.

**She stands in four fielded comps**: Tower of Eternity, Darkswarm Invasion, Battle Drills Team 4 and the Ravaged Realm Aurora Squad. She is also the one hero the **Enlightening Spell +10** aims at correctly, because she is the only back-row hero in Battle Drills Team 4. See [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md).
