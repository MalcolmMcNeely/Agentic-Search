# Cyran

> **Covers:** Cyran kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

- **Faction:** Lightbearer
- **Class:** Mage
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 6
- **Level:** S

## Kit

Ultimate Gravitic Requiem opens a black hole that damages and pulls all enemies to the centre, instantly killing anything below 15% HP. Cursed Grasp controls and Arcane Barrage hits an area.

## Best for

Campaign and PvE, and strong in PvP. Crowd control plus high damage.
