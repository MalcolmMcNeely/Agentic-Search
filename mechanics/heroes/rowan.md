# Rowan

> **Covers:** Rowan kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

- **Faction:** Lightbearer
- **Class:** Mage
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 3
- **Level:** A

## Kit

Ultimate lets him move while throwing coins that restore Energy to nearby allies. His trolley drops health and energy potions that automatically heal teammates below 50% HP and buff their defence.

## Best for

Campaign and PvP support. An Energy battery rather than a damage dealer.
