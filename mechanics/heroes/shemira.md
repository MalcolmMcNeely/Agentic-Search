# Shemira

> **Covers:** Shemira kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-02

- **Faction:** Graveborn
- **Class:** Mage
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 4
- **Rarity:** S

## Kit

| Skill | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Phantom Procession, Ultimate | summons **10 ghosts for 10s**. Each ghost deals 110% + 15% damage, and heals her for 50% + 5% of the damage it deals. Every cast resets the strike count |
| Sacrificial Orb, Skill 1 | spends 10% of her current HP on an orb that travels forward for 320% + 40% damage |
| Ghastly Tribute, Skill 2 | generates ghosts when allies lose HP worth 50% of her max HP. Used actively, it spends 15% of her HP to deal **true damage worth 24% + 3% of the enemy's max HP** |
| Hero Focus, **Legendary+** | **+24% Energy recovery from attacks**, up to 40%, plus **6% more per 3 ghosts** on the field |
| Exclusive Equipment, **Mythic+** | Spectral Barrier. See below |
| Enhance Force, **Supreme+** | summons **one extra ghost** whenever an enemy dies during her Ultimate |

**She heals herself off her own damage, which is why she survives without a healer.** Four comps in [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md) field her with no dedicated healer for exactly this reason.

## Exclusive Equipment: Spectral Barrier

Unlocks at **Mythic+**, and this account holds her at **Supreme with the weapon at +1**. See [../equipment-and-charms.md](../equipment-and-charms.md).

**The base skill is a shield that becomes a bomb.** When she has taken 30% of her max HP in damage, or when her ghosts reach their cap, she sacrifices one ghost for a shield that absorbs **250%** damage for 5s. When it expires she throws whatever is left of it at every enemy as damage.

| EX level | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| +5 | shield rises to **270%** |
| **+10** | during her Ultimate, every ghost hit deals extra **true damage worth 3% of the target's max HP**, capped at 90%, and **cuts the target's max HP** by that amount |
| +15 | shield rises to **300%** |

**+10 is the step worth saving for, and it is the best EX level available anywhere on this account.** The +5 and +15 steps buy shield percentage. The +10 step buys defence-ignoring damage on a hero who fires 10 ghosts for 10s and has that Ultimate cloned by Rolan for 10 more. [../../account/progression.md](../../account/progression.md) argues that on the uncapped Infinite Stages ladder, damage that ignores defence is the only kind that never stops working. **She is Supreme, so +10 is reachable**: levels 6 to 10 need Supreme and she has it.

The bill is **600 Tidal Essence** for +2 to +5, then **375 Temporal Essence** for +6 to +10. See [../../account/equipment.md](../../account/equipment.md).

## Tier gate

**Every gated skill she has is on except one.** Hero Focus came at Legendary+ and Spectral Barrier at Mythic+, and Mythic+ is the only tier in the game that adds a skill. **Enhance Force needs Supreme+ and she is one step short**, so the extra ghost on an enemy death is off. That is the smallest of the three, because it pays only where enemies die during her Ultimate. See [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md) and [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).

**She is one of four heroes on the account at Supreme or above**, alongside Taichi & Agumon, Eironn and Yamato & Gabumon, and the only Graveborn among them.

## Best for

Campaign and PvE. Sustained damage with built-in survivability, and the account's **scaling carry**: the longer a fight runs, the steeper her curve. See [../team-archetypes.md](../team-archetypes.md).

**PvP splits hard between the two independent lists.** `unverified`: **S+** [allclash, 2026-08-26, references Mythic+, Supreme+ and EX priority as its investment band] against **absent from the top tier** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. Both readings stand. See [../tier-list-sources.md](../tier-list-sources.md), which records this as one of the six sharpest disagreements between those two sources.

**No current list rates her for Legend Trial, Arcane Labyrinth or Battle Drills**, and those are three of the five modes this account fields her in. Her placement there comes from her kit, per [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md).

## Against this account

**She holds four comps at once**: Tower of Eternity, Skyclops, Darkswarm Invasion and solo battles. A change to her tier or kit moves all four. **She is queued as a late swap into the Arena comp**, against enemy shields only, and the cost there is the whole Dimensional faction core. See [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md).

**She is not a Hand of Resonance, and it costs her nothing.** All five Hands stand at 210, so the Resonance Level is 210 and she runs at 210 like every other hero on the account. Borrowing a slot for her buys nothing while that holds. See [../../account/progression.md](../../account/progression.md).

**Rolan doubles her, so every point of investment in her is doubled too.** His clone casts her Ultimate again for 10 more ghosts. It is the largest single damage add the account owns, and it copies her Supreme tier and her EX weapon with it.

**She is the hero queued to answer the push comp stalling.** The current push comp gave up two of its four defence-ignoring damage taps, and Ghastly Tribute is the one that was lost. She is now **Supreme with a weapon** rather than Mythic, so the swap costs less than it did. See [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md).
