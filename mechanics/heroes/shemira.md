# Shemira

> **Covers:** Shemira kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

- **Faction:** Graveborn
- **Class:** Mage
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 4
- **Level:** S

## Kit

Ultimate summons 10 ghosts to bombard enemies for 10s, each ghost healing her for part of its damage. The strike count resets on every Ultimate. A passive summons ghosts when allies take heavy damage, and ghosts can be sacrificed for true damage scaled to enemy max HP plus a shield.

## Best for

Campaign and PvE. Sustained damage with built-in survivability.
