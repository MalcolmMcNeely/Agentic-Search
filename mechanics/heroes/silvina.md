# Silvina

> **Covers:** Silvina kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-08

- **Faction:** Graveborn
- **Class:** Rogue
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 1
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

She is an opener. Everything she does happens in the first seconds of the fight, and almost none of it happens after.

**First Strike** marks the closest enemy in a symmetrical position, flashes her next to them, and hits for **300%** at battle start. That teleport crosses the grid before either side has moved, so it reaches the enemy backline that a range-1 Rogue could never walk to.

**Blade Vortex** turns her normal attack into **Whirl Assault** for the first **12s**, each hit dealing **140%**. Killing the marked enemy extends that window.

**Shadow Slayer**, her Ultimate, deals **300%** to an enemy and cuts **200 Energy** off them.

## The level-up upgrades are all live, and that changes her numbers

Her four skills each gain upgrades at hero levels 71, 91, 131, 151, 191 and 211. **This account sits at Resonance Level 240**, so **every one of them is on**. See [../../account/progression.md](../../account/progression.md).

`unverified`: the thresholds and their values come from afk.global alone. yaphalla lists the skills without the level table and prydwen returns 403. Read them off her skill screen.

| Skill | Base | At 240 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First Strike | 300% | **340%, plus a 5s stun** |
| Blade Vortex, per Whirl Assault | 140% | **170%** |
| Shadow Slayer, Energy cut | 200 | **300** |

**The 5s stun is the biggest of these.** It lands on the enemy she teleports to, at battle start, before anyone has cast anything. Five seconds of one enemy not acting is survival by the reckoning in [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md), and it is the front half of a burst window for the rest of the team.

## Best for

**Energy denial, and she is the roster's second source of it.** Shadow Slayer removes 300 Energy from one enemy and the opening stun removes 5s of Energy gain on top. Viperian starves the whole enemy team 20 at a time; Silvina takes one target's bar apart. An enemy that never fills its bar never casts its Ultimate. See [../team-archetypes.md](../team-archetypes.md) and [viperian.md](viperian.md).

**She picks the target, and that is rare on this roster.** First Strike reaches a symmetrical position, so where she stands decides who she jumps on. That makes her a counter-pick rather than a default: bring her when one named enemy hero is the fight. See [../formation.md](../formation.md).

**Honor Duel is her mode, and the reason is in the patch notes rather than in a tier list.** Version 1.7.3 gives her **+20% damage dealt and 10% less damage taken inside Honor Duel only**, the largest positive modifier in that table. She is also Graveborn, one of the four factions the mode's shop stocks. **That modifier beats any tier letter here**, because no source has ranked Honor Duel since 2024-04-04 and the 1.7.3 re-tune was aimed at this mode alone. See [../honor-duel.md](../honor-duel.md).

**She is worst against a boss.** Her whole kit is single-target burst aimed at an enemy that has an Energy bar and can be stunned, and a Dream Realm boss shrugs off control and outlasts a 12s window.

## Tier gate

**She stands at Epic, and three of her six pieces are dark.**

- **Hero Focus**, **+24 Crit** for the whole battle, needs **Legendary+**, three steps up.
- **Shield of Light** gives her a shield blocking **450%** damage after First Strike. It is an **Exclusive Equipment gated at Mythic+**, five steps up.
- **Enhance Force** makes each Whirl Assault cut the target's **Vitality by 60 for 3s**. It needs **Supreme+**.

**Shield of Light is the one that decides whether she is playable in PvP, and it is five steps away.** First Strike drops a range-1 Rogue alone in the enemy formation at second zero. With the shield she survives the answer; without it she trades herself for one stun. That is the whole of allclash's warning about ascension.

She is **A-Level**, so the **Dream Store stocks her copies** at 1,200 Dream Fragments each. Epic to Legendary+ is 4 + 5 + 8 = **17 Soul Sigils**. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md).
