# Chippy

> **Covers:** Chippy kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

- **Faction:** Lightbearer
- **Class:** Tank
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 1
- **Level:** Rare

## Kit

Summons hamster allies to attack while he rides on their shoulders. Normal attacks rarely trigger a very strong hit, and he can leap to an enemy for instant damage.

## Best for

Tank filler only, rated C tier.

## Notes

Cannot ascend. afk.global's page labels him A Level; the account holds him at Rare and the account is the record.
