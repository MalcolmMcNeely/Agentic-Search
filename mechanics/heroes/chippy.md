# Chippy

> **Covers:** Chippy kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-31

- **Faction:** Lightbearer
- **Class:** Tank
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 1
- **Rarity:** Rare

## Kit

Summons hamster allies to attack while he rides on their shoulders. Normal attacks rarely trigger a very strong hit, and he can leap to an enemy for instant damage.

## Rare is a rarity, not a tier letter

**`Rarity: Rare` is what he is pulled as, and it is on a different scale from every tier letter in this folder.** The gacha scale runs S-Level, A-Level, Rare. The ascension scale in [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md) runs Elite to Supreme+. A tier list letter is a third scale again, an opinion about performance in one mode. Nothing converts between them. See [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md) and [../tier-list-sources.md](../tier-list-sources.md).

## Best for

**No current vetted tier list ranks him in any mode.** allclash, afk.global and Pocket Tactics all cover 115 heroes or more, and he appears on none of them. That is guide silence, and guide silence is not evidence of weakness: it means he sits outside what those lists chose to cover. His placement is scored from the kit instead, per [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md).

**Time alive.** He is a range-1 body that stands in the front row and absorbs hits aimed at the carry. That is the whole of his defensive contribution: no shield, no heal, no damage reduction, no taunt and no control.

**Damage out.** His hamsters are extra bodies attacking, the rare heavy proc adds burst that cannot be relied on, and the leap is a single hit. All of it is small.

**So he is an early-game frontline body, and a summon source.** He pays thinly into both sums and never stops paying thinly, because he cannot be raised.

`?` Whether his hamsters count for **Aurora**, whose Hero Focus reads allied summons belonging to a different ally. Her file lists Zanie's turrets as feeding it; no source says either way about hamsters. One battle settles it. See [aurora.md](aurora.md).

## Tier gate

**Rare heroes cannot ascend at all**, so he never holds an ascension tier, never reaches **Legendary+** for Hero Focus, and never reaches **Mythic+** for Exclusive Equipment. He has no ceiling to build toward, which is the real limit on him and a firmer statement than any letter would be.

afk.global's character page labels him A Level. The account holds him at Rare, and the account is the record. See [../../CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md).
