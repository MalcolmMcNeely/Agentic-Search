# Faramor

> **Covers:** Faramor kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-09

- **Faction:** Wilder
- **Class:** Rogue
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 1
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

| Skill | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Sanctified Circle, Ultimate | a magic circle dealing **210% true damage**, then continuous damage that rises with the HP each enemy has already lost. **Enemies inside cannot heal** |
| Glory's Embrace, CD 10s | a shield absorbing **200%** for 6s, plus **250%** to his current target. **It deals true damage instead if an ally is currently buffing his stats** |
| Sacred Pledge | blesses one **adjacent** ally. Both he and the blessed hero gain **12% ATK**, reapplied on expiry, and every 3s he deals 100% to enemies within 1 tile of either of them and **stuns them 0.5s** |
| Hero Focus, **Legendary+** | **+12 Haste** for the whole battle, rising to 16 and 20 with Tidal Essence |
| Bitter Requiem, **Mythic+** | see below |
| Enhance Force, **Supreme+** | an enemy that revives inside the circle loses **50 Vitality** |

`unverified`: the upgrade thresholds. afk.global lists Glory's Embrace rising at Lv.71, 131 and 191, which are hero levels, but lists Sacred Pledge and the Ultimate rising at single- and double-digit numbers, which are not. The scale is not stated. The base values above are agreed by both sources and are what a comp should be built on.

## Exclusive Equipment: Bitter Requiem

Unlocks at **Mythic+**, and this account holds him a tier past that at **Supreme**.

**It turns his circle into a team-wide true damage source, and that is a different hero from the one the tier lists rate.** Inside the circle, **every ally deals extra true damage equal to 75% of their own ATK**, rising to 85% at the top of the weapon. The circle also lets him cast Glory's Embrace in place of his normal attack, with the shield cap raised to 600%, and it **widens by 2 tiles once per battle** after he defeats an enemy.

**Read the ally clause carefully: it pays the whole comp, not him.** Every other true damage source on the roster is one hero's own damage. This one multiplies four other heroes at once, so it is worth more the more ATK stands beside him.

**This is the single best fit on the account for the argument in [../../account/progression.md](../../account/progression.md).** Infinite Stages inflate enemy defence with no ceiling, and true damage ignores defence entirely. Bitter Requiem converts a slice of the whole team's ATK into the one kind of damage the ladder cannot resist.

**The weapon stands at +1.** Supreme opens it to **+10**, so nine levels can still be bought: **600 Tidal Essence** clears +2 to +5, then **375 Temporal Essence** clears the rest. That is the same bill Shemira and Bonnie each carry, so price no longer separates the three. See [../../account/equipment.md](../../account/equipment.md).

## Best for

A **boss amplify** engine as well as a payload, and a **heal-block answer**: the circle stops enemy healing outright, which is what makes him a push tool against comps that out-sustain the team.

**Campaign, rated S** [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated], **S** [afk.global Campaign tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated] and **S** in story mode [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. All three current lists land on the same letter, which makes this the best-evidenced letter on the account.

**Dream Realm Endless, rated S** [afk.global Dream Realm Endless tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated]. His Ultimate deals true damage and his periodic damage reads HP already lost, and Endless pays for exactly those two things. See [../game-modes.md](../game-modes.md).

**Dream Realm** `unverified`: **A+** [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated], **A** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated] and **B** [afk.global Dream Realm tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated].

**PvP** `unverified`, and this is the widest split on him, five bands: **S** [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated], **B** [afk.global PvP tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated] and **C** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. All three readings stand. allclash attaches a condition to its own S that the other two would not: it says he "needs manual play" and does not work in every setup, and PvP defence is fought without a player at the controls.

## Tier gate

**Every skill he has is live.** He stands at **Supreme**, so Hero Focus came at Legendary+ and Bitter Requiem at Mythic+. **Only Enhance Force is left, at Supreme+, one step up**, and it is a footnote on enemies that revive.

**That clears the question the letters above could not answer.** No current list states whether it rates him with the EX weapon on. Now that the weapon is on this copy, **the highest of the three readings is the one describing this account's hero**, not a ceiling it cannot reach. See [../tier-list-sources.md](../tier-list-sources.md) and [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).

He is **A-Level**, and **Supreme+ costs 20 Soul Sigils**. **The Dream Store does not stock him**, so those copies can only come off the All-Hero banner, and the step buys one footnote skill plus **+5** on the Resonance Synergy cap, where levels past 300 pay Diamonds rather than stats. **Do not chase it.** See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md) and [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md).

`?` **where his copies came from.** He is absent from the Dream Store shelf, so the 12 sigils to Mythic+ and the 200 Faction Acorns to Supreme were paid somewhere the repo does not record. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md).

## Notes

**Two formation rules ride on him, and they now pull the same way.** Sacred Pledge buffs **one adjacent ally**, and Bitter Requiem pays **every ally standing in the circle**. So the comp wants to be built around his tile, not spread away from it. See [../formation.md](../formation.md).

**Glory's Embrace turning true is a second reason to stand a buffer next to him.** It converts to true damage whenever an ally is buffing his stats, so a Hugin, a Solise or an Elijah & Lailah beside him upgrades the damage type of his own attack as well.
