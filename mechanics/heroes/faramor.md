# Faramor

> **Covers:** Faramor kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-01

- **Faction:** Wilder
- **Class:** Rogue
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 1
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

Ultimate summons a magic circle dealing true damage and blocking enemy healing. Enemies inside take periodic damage scaled to HP already lost. Sacred Pledge buffs himself and one adjacent ally by 12 to 15% ATK and damages nearby enemies every 3s.

## Best for

A support damage dealer against grouped enemies, and a **heal-block answer**: the circle stops enemy healing outright, which is what makes him a push tool against comps that out-sustain the team.

**Campaign, rated S** [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated], **S** [afk.global Campaign tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated] and **S** in story mode [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. All three current lists land on the same letter, which makes this the best-evidenced letter on the account.

**Dream Realm Endless, rated S** [afk.global Dream Realm Endless tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated]. His Ultimate deals true damage and his periodic damage reads HP already lost, and Endless pays for exactly those two things. See [../game-modes.md](../game-modes.md).

**Dream Realm** `unverified`: **A+** [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated], **A** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated] and **B** [afk.global Dream Realm tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated].

**PvP** `unverified`, and this is the widest split on him, five bands: **S** [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated], **B** [afk.global PvP tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated] and **C** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. All three readings stand. allclash attaches a condition to its own S that the other two would not: it says he "needs manual play" and does not work in every setup, and PvP defence is fought without a player at the controls.

## Tier gate

**He is Mythic, so Hero Focus is live.** Exclusive Equipment needs **Mythic+**, **one ascension up**, and no current list states whether its letters assume it. That single step is what would settle every `unverified` letter above, because a source rating him at his ceiling is rating a hero with the EX weapon on.

He is **A-Level**, so the Dream Store stocks his copies and the step is a Dream Fragment purchase rather than a banner pull. **Mythic to Mythic+ costs 12 Soul Sigils**, which is 14,400 fragments. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md), [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md) and [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).
