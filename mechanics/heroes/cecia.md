# Cecia

> **Covers:** Cecia kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

- **Faction:** Graveborn
- **Class:** Marksman
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 5
- **Level:** S

## Kit

Ultimate Queen's Summons deploys Mr. Carlyle, an ally who inherits her stats and hits in an arc. He bleeds HP over time and can be resummoned to heal. Trial of Thorns immobilises and steals 1.5% of the target's Phys DEF and Magic DEF every second, kept for the whole fight. Agonizing Puncture gives passive DEF penetration and stronger normal attacks after three hits.

## Best for

General play, rated S, and campaign, rated B. The account's strongest Graveborn damage dealer.
