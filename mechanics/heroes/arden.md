## Kit

Ultimate Force of Nature picks a tile and calls lightning on every enemy within 2 tiles, leaving a lasting dark cloud. Entangling Vines immobilises two nearby foes and deals rolling damage. Nature's Resilience returns Energy while enemies are controlled.

## Best for

Campaign. Crowd control plus steady magic damage.
