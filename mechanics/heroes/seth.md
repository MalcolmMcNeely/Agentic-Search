# Seth

> **Covers:** Seth kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

- **Faction:** Mauler
- **Class:** Rogue
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 1
- **Level:** A

## Kit

Shadow Strike dashes between enemies for repeated hits then a final leap that grants invincibility and HP recovery. Bloodlust stacks raise Haste, Life Drain and damage.

## Best for

Fights with many killable enemies, because a kill resets his cooldown and returns Energy.

## Notes

Weaker in campaign, boss and PvP.
