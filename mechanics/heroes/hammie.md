# Hammie

> **Covers:** Hammie kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

- **Faction:** Lightbearer
- **Class:** Support
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 5
- **Level:** Rare

## Kit

Ultimate Pretty Fireball throws a charged projectile for up to 340% damage. Heals the weakest ally and raises their ATK, and self-heals below 70% HP.

## Best for

Sustain support only.

## Notes

Cannot ascend, so she falls off fast. afk.global's page labels her A Level; the account holds her at Rare and the account is the record.
