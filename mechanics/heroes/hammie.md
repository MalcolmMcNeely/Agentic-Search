# Hammie

> **Covers:** Hammie kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-31

- **Faction:** Lightbearer
- **Class:** Support
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 5
- **Rarity:** Rare

## Kit

Ultimate Pretty Fireball throws a charged projectile for up to 340% damage. Heals the weakest ally and raises their ATK, and self-heals below 70% HP.

## Rare is a rarity, not a tier letter

**`Rarity: Rare` is what she is pulled as, and it is on a different scale from every tier letter in this folder.** The gacha scale runs S-Level, A-Level, Rare. The ascension scale in [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md) runs Elite to Supreme+. A tier list letter is a third scale again, an opinion about performance in one mode. Nothing converts between them. See [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md) and [../tier-list-sources.md](../tier-list-sources.md).

## Best for

**No current vetted tier list ranks her in any mode.** allclash, afk.global and Pocket Tactics all cover 115 heroes or more, and she appears on none of them. That is guide silence, and guide silence is not evidence of weakness: it means she sits outside what those lists chose to cover. Her placement is scored from the kit instead, per [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md).

**Time alive.** A heal on the weakest ally, plus a self-heal under 70% HP. Single-target and reactive, so it buys seconds for one hero rather than the team, and it stops no burst.

**Damage out.** An ATK buff riding on that heal, so it lands on whoever is lowest rather than on the carry, and a 340% Ultimate from range 5.

**So she is a single-target sustain slot for early content.** Both halves of her kit aim at the weakest ally, which is the wrong target for the ATK buff and the right one for the heal.

## Tier gate

**Rare heroes cannot ascend at all**, so she never holds an ascension tier, never reaches **Legendary+** for Hero Focus, and never reaches **Mythic+** for Exclusive Equipment. She has no ceiling to build toward, which is why she falls off rather than scaling.

afk.global's character page labels her A Level. The account holds her at Rare, and the account is the record. See [../../CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md).
