# Yamato & Gabumon

> **Covers:** Yamato & Gabumon kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-31

- **Faction:** Dimensional
- **Class:** Mage
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** not published
- **Level:** S

## Kit

Digivolves into MetalGarurumon on the first Ultimate. Ultimate Cocytus Breath leaps onto the target, freezes the surrounding area and applies continuous HP loss. Garuru Tomahawk is a large freeze missile with splash damage. Garuru Howling stacks ATK and Haste on himself. Grace Cross Freezer is a spread missile barrage that hits several enemies at once, and repeat hits on the same target do less, so it spreads rather than deleting one hero.

Yamato cheers Gabumon with an ATK boost at battle start and again on every Ultimate cast, plus extra Energy recovery until the digivolve lands.

## Best for

Dream Realm and AFK Stages. Backline magic damage and crowd control, and the heaviest freeze on the roster once digivolved.

## Notes

More fragile than Agumon, so keep him in the back row. Pairs directly with Taichi & Agumon: at battle start WarGreymon hands every ally behind him +25% of his own ATK for 20s, and Gabumon taking that buff gains 300 Energy, which shortens his own ramp. Also works with Elijah & Lailah, Rowan and Hugin.
