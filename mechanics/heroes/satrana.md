# Satrana

> **Covers:** Satrana kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-10

- **Faction:** Mauler
- **Class:** Mage
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 1
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

| Skill | Gate | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fiery Dance, Ultimate | none | **240% damage** to enemies within **2 tiles**, and she is **invincible while it casts**. From level 51 the last strike **charms for 2s**, so the target can only normal-attack |
| Vixen Rush | none | scratches a 1-tile arc **twice**, each hit **110% damage plus 2.5% of the target's max HP**, and grants her **100 Life Drain**. The max-HP part is **capped at 75% of her own ATK** |
| Ignite Passions | none | at battle start, allies within **2 tiles** gain **Sparks**. Once Satrana or a Sparked ally has damaged a target **3 times**, that target is **ignited** and burns for **0.8% of its max HP per second** |
| Hero Focus | **Legendary+** | **12% less damage taken** for the whole battle |
| Radiant Vigor, Exclusive Equipment | **Mythic+** | while any enemy is ignited she takes **16% less magic damage** for the rest of the battle, and shares it with a nearby ally once a second enemy ignites. **+5** pays her **200 Energy** per ignited enemy, **+10** raises the reduction to 20%, **+15** to 22% |
| Enhance Force | **Supreme+** | **Vixen Rush loses its cooldown** once every enemy is ignited, or once 5 enemies have been |

## Tier gate

**She is Epic, so three of her six pieces are live and three are dark.** Fiery Dance, Vixen Rush and Ignite Passions all work. **Hero Focus, Radiant Vigor and Enhance Force do not.** See [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).

**All three dark pieces are the ones that keep her alive.** Hero Focus is flat damage reduction, Radiant Vigor is magic damage reduction, and Enhance Force is the uptime that would let her drain faster than she is hit. A range-1 hero with none of them standing in the front row is the version this account fields.

**She is A-Level and she is on the Dream Store shelf**, so her copies cost Dream Fragments rather than banner sigils. Epic to Legendary+ is **17 Soul Sigils** and switches Hero Focus on. Mythic+, which opens Radiant Vigor, is **39** from here. At 1,200 Fragments a copy that is a long road. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md).

## Best for

**Pocket Tactics ranks her C for PvP**, on a page dated 2026-09-09, under its own heading "AFK Journey tier list - PVP". It ranks her **C for story mode** on the same page. **It states no investment level**, which is that source's standing weakness. **It leaves her off its Dream Realm list entirely.** allclash does not name her at all, which is guide silence and says nothing either way. See [../tier-list-sources.md](../tier-list-sources.md) and [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md).

**Damage out, and it is the kind this account keeps asking for.** Ignite burns **0.8% of the target's max HP every second**, and a percentage of HP ignores enemy defence completely. On Infinite Stages, where enemy defence climbs with no ceiling and the roster's ATK does not, that is the only kind of damage that holds its value. It is the same argument that put Starshard Spell in the push comp. See [../artifacts.md](../artifacts.md).

**Ignite Passions is a team skill, not a personal one.** Sparks go to every ally within 2 tiles at battle start, and any Sparked ally's hits count toward the 3 needed to ignite. So a bunched comp ignites in the opening seconds and a spread one may never ignite at all. That makes her an **amplifier** whose output is set by where the other four stand.

**Vixen Rush does not scale the same way.** Its 2.5% of max HP is capped at 75% of her ATK, so against a very high-HP enemy the cap bites and the percentage stops paying. Only the ignite tick is uncapped.

**Survival is Life Drain and one invincibility window, and both are thin at Epic.** Vixen Rush grants **100 Life Drain**, and Fiery Dance makes her untouchable while it casts. Nothing else protects her, because the three pieces that would are dark.

**So she is a damage-over-time amplifier for a bunched comp, and a front-row liability until Legendary+.** See [../team-archetypes.md](../team-archetypes.md).

**Range 1 is what places her, and it fights her class label.** She is a Mage, so the default row is the back, but a base range of 1 overrides the label and puts her in the front. Fiery Dance also wants enemies within 2 tiles of her, and Ignite Passions wants allies within 2 tiles, so she has to stand in the middle of both. See [../formation.md](../formation.md).

**She is fielded in no comp.** Every comp in [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md) already holds a higher-tier hero in her lane, and the Mauler slots in particular are held by Galahad at Mythic and Smokey & Meerky at Supreme.
