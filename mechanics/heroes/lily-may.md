# Lily May

> **Covers:** Lily May kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-31

- **Faction:** Wilder
- **Class:** Rogue
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 3
- **Rarity:** S

## Kit

Her Ultimate Tempest Shot is a **passive**. She enters a Defensive State at battle start, and answers with Quellbolt attacks whenever a non-summoned enemy casts an Ultimate. Rainstorm Ballet dashes to a target for 6 scaling hits. Swallow's Flight grows her through normal attacks and skills into enhanced attacks dealing true damage off the target's max HP. Rainfall Bliss grows her ATK each time an ally buffs her stats. Hero Focus adds Penetration, more of it during Rainstorm Ballet.

## Best for

She is a **counter pick, not a general carry**: her Ultimate only answers enemy Ultimates, so she punishes teams that lean on them, including enemy Eironn teams. Bring her when the enemy comp is Ultimate-reliant. See [../team-archetypes.md](../team-archetypes.md).

**Every mode splits, and PvP splits widest.** The kit explains why: against a team that casts Ultimates she is a counter, and against one that does not she has no Ultimate of her own.

**PvP** `unverified`: rated **S+** [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated], **A** [afk.global PvP tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated] and **B** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. All three readings stand.

**Campaign** `unverified`: **S** as PvE [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated], **A** [afk.global Campaign tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated] and **B** in story mode [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated].

**Dream Realm** `unverified`, and it is the widest gap on her: **A** [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated], **C** [afk.global Dream Realm tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated] and **D** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. Two of the three put her near the bottom, and the kit agrees with them: a Dream Realm boss is one enemy whose Ultimate cadence she cannot rely on.

**Dream Realm Endless, rated B** [afk.global Dream Realm Endless tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated], one band above its blended Dream Realm reading, which her true damage off enemy max HP would explain.

allclash reads her as a hero past her peak: strong at release in Dream Realm and against Eironn comps, and today primarily a PvP answer to control comps rather than a top-shelf pick.

## Tier gate

**She is Epic, so the Hero Focus in her kit line is off.** It unlocks at **Legendary+**, three ascensions up, and it is the Penetration half of her damage. **Exclusive Equipment needs Mythic+**, five ascensions up. No current list states an investment level, so the S+ describes a copy with both of those live.

She is **S-Level**, so she starts at Epic and her copies do not come from the Dream Store. See [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md), [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md) and [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).
