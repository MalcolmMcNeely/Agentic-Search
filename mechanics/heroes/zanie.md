# Zanie

> **Covers:** Zanie kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

- **Faction:** Lightbearer
- **Class:** Marksman
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 20
- **Level:** A

## Kit

Deploys two laser turrets that fire at enemies in range and can link for a combined attack. Iron Defense builds a gun turret that charges then unloads a barrage. Field Repairs restores damaged turrets with shields.

## Best for

Campaign and boss, both A+. Sustained turret damage.

## Notes

Weak in PvP.
