# Koko

> **Covers:** Koko kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-01

- **Faction:** Mauler
- **Class:** Support
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 4
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

Ultimate Full Energy gives allies 45 to 55% damage reduction for 12s, then converts everything blocked into true damage against enemies. Fulfilling Feast heals the weakest ally and raises their ATK.

## Tier gate

**She is Legendary, one step short of Legendary+ and her Hero Focus.** That step costs **8 Soul Sigils**, and the Dream Store stocks her, so it is a Dream Fragment purchase rather than a banner pull. See [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md), [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md) and [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).

## Best for

Campaign and PvP. Damage mitigation plus healing.

**A team-wide mitigation is worth five heroes' survival bought with one slot**, and the conversion to true damage means the same cooldown also pays into the damage sum. That is why she scores above a single-target Warrior a tier higher. See [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md).
