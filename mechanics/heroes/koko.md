# Koko

> **Covers:** Koko kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-09

- **Faction:** Mauler
- **Class:** Support
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 4
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

Ultimate Full Energy gives every ally **45 to 55% damage reduction for 12s**, records everything it blocks, and then deals that total back as **true damage**. It also hands out Life Drain and **10% ATK**, and it cannot be dispelled or recast while it runs.

Fulfilling Feast heals the weakest ally for up to **260%** and gives them **10% ATK for 5s**, and separately feeds Patto for **350%** HP every 15s. Patto, Smash! throws a spear for up to **240%**, raises that target's **damage taken by 10% for 5s**, and ground-slams anything adjacent for a **3s stun** on an 8s cooldown.

## Tier gate

**She is Legendary+, so Hero Focus is live: +12 Haste for the whole battle.** Haste is the stat her kit wants most, because her Ultimate is the whole hero and Haste is what brings it back sooner. More casts of Full Energy means more seconds of team-wide mitigation and more blocked damage converted into true damage, so the passive multiplies the skill she is fielded for. See [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md).

**Two skills are still locked.**

- **Fluffy Shield**, an **Exclusive Equipment gated at Mythic+**, shields allies for 15% of the damage they deal over 7s, rising to 20% at EX +5 and stripping control at EX +10.
- **Enhance Force**, at **Supreme+**, adds 25 Vitality for 6s after Fulfilling Feast.

Mythic+ is three steps up. The Dream Store stocks her, so those steps are a Dream Fragment purchase rather than a banner pull. See [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md), [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md) and [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).

## Best for

Campaign and PvP. Damage mitigation plus healing.

**A team-wide mitigation is worth five heroes' survival bought with one slot**, and the conversion to true damage means the same cooldown also pays into the damage sum. That is why she scores above a single-target Warrior a tier higher. See [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md).

**True damage is the account's stated need.** Infinite Stages inflate enemy defence with no cap, and true damage ignores defence entirely, so Koko turns a defensive cooldown into offence that the ladder cannot resist. See [../../account/progression.md](../../account/progression.md).

**PvP, rated S** [allclash, 2026-08-26, investment not stated], confirmed on a re-read of that page. The kit argument above stands without it.

**She holds the sustain slot in the Arena defence**, and she is the first swap into the second attack shape if that comp gets deleted by one enemy Ultimate rather than out-damaged. She is also the standing swap on the Tower of Will comp. She is Mauler, so the cost is the faction cap and nothing else.

**A wide enemy Ultimate is what puts her ahead of a bigger healer on defence.** Full Energy reads no tile, so the team can spread as far as the rows allow, where an aroma or a circle pays only to allies bunched around one tile. Against Gaia Force, which hits 7 times within 3 tiles, that spacing buys the defence seconds, and seconds are the whole of a defence's job.

**That argument does not carry into an attack, and the account's record says so.** An attacker has to kill all five inside the clock, so it wins the opening rather than survives it, and the running attack comp bunches on purpose to cast one larger area Ultimate first. Mitigation does not speed that up. See [../pvp-team-building.md](../pvp-team-building.md) and [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md).

**Arena flatters her, because it caps hero levels at 151 plus about 10% and leaves unlocked skills alone.** She is **Legendary+**, so her Hero Focus is live, which is more than three of the five heroes in attack A can say. Her mitigation is also a percentage, so the cap does not shrink it. See [../arena.md](../arena.md) and [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md).
