# Hugin

> **Covers:** Hugin kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-31

- **Faction:** Lightbearer
- **Class:** Support
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 20
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

Ultimate places an anvil anchor and forges gear that raises the top damage dealer's ATK and Haste. Core skills put cogshields on allies and give nearby teammates damage reduction and ATK.

## Best for

He pays into both sums at once: cogshields and damage reduction buy time alive, the forged ATK and Haste buy damage out, and the Ultimate aims that at whoever the team's top damage dealer already is. He wants **static ranged allies** standing near him, because the nearby-teammate half of his kit is positional. See [../formation.md](../formation.md) and [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md).

**Campaign, rated S+** [afk.global Campaign tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated], with **S** as PvE [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated] and **S** in story mode [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. All three sit in the same letter, so this is his firmest reading.

**Dream Realm** `unverified`: **S** [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated], **A+** [afk.global Dream Realm tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated] and **A** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. A single armoured boss is the fight his top-damage-dealer buff aims at cleanly, which is the case for the higher reading.

**Dream Realm Endless, rated A+** [afk.global Dream Realm Endless tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated].

**PvP** `unverified`, his weakest mode: **A** [afk.global PvP tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated], **B+** [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated] and **B** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. His kit ramps through an Ultimate and a forge, and Arena is settled before that lands.

## Tier gate

**He is Mythic, so Hero Focus, at Legendary+, is live.** Exclusive Equipment needs **Mythic+**, one ascension up, and no current list states whether its letters assume it.

He is **A-Level**, so the Dream Store stocks his copies and that step is a Dream Fragment purchase rather than a banner pull. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md), [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md) and [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).
