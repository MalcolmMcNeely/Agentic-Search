# Hugin

> **Covers:** Hugin kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-11

- **Faction:** Lightbearer
- **Class:** Support
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 20
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

**Unstoppable!**, his Ultimate, anchors him to an **anvil** for the battle and forges a **Gear of Might** onto the **highest damage dealer**, raising that hero's ATK by **6 to 10% of Hugin's own base ATK** and Haste by **80 for 10s**.

**Titan's Aegis** puts a **cogshield** on the weakest ally that blocks **600% damage for 8s**, up to 660% at this account's level. **Mechanized Bond** plants a sigil behind him that gives nearby allies ATK worth **14 to 20% of his base ATK**, and hands allies an extra **75 Energy** whenever Titan's Aegis fires.

## Tier gate

**He is Supreme, so Hero Focus and his Exclusive Equipment are both on, and only Enhance Force is dark.**

**Hero Focus**, from Legendary+, grants **Haste during battle: 12 at level 1, rising to 20**. `?` which of the three levels this account holds. They are bought with **Tidal Essence**, so the tier does not settle it.

**Steelbound Kinship is the Exclusive Equipment skill, and it is now live.** When Unstoppable! fires, he forges cogshields onto the **2 weakest allies** that block **800% damage for 8s**, rising to 900% with weapon levels, and higher weapon levels add **control immunity** while those shields hold.

**Enhance Force needs Supreme+**, one tier up. Allies under a cogshield would then take **30% less damage** as well.

`?` **His Exclusive Equipment level.** At Supreme the weapon's ceiling is **+10**. He is **A-Level**, and the Dream Store stocks his copies, so further tiers are a Dream Fragment purchase rather than a banner pull. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md), [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md) and [../../account/equipment.md](../../account/equipment.md).

**No current tier list states whether its letters assume that weapon**, and every letter below was published while he stood without it.

## Best for

He pays into both sums at once: cogshields and damage reduction buy time alive, the forged ATK and Haste buy damage out, and the Ultimate aims that at whoever the team's top damage dealer already is. He wants **static ranged allies** standing near him, because the nearby-teammate half of his kit is positional. See [../formation.md](../formation.md) and [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md).

**Steelbound Kinship doubles the shielded count, so his formation job has changed.** Titan's Aegis covers the weakest ally; the weapon covers the **two** weakest each time the Ultimate lands. A comp fielding two fragile carries now keeps both of them.
