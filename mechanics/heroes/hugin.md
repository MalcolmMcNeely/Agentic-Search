# Hugin

> **Covers:** Hugin kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

- **Faction:** Lightbearer
- **Class:** Support
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 20
- **Level:** A

## Kit

Ultimate places an anvil anchor and forges gear that raises the top damage dealer's ATK and Haste. Core skills put cogshields on allies and give nearby teammates damage reduction and ATK.

## Best for

Campaign, rated S+, and boss fights. Defensive support plus damage amplification.
