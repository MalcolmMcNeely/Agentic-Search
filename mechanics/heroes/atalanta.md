# Atalanta

> **Covers:** Atalanta kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-07

- **Faction:** Lightbearer
- **Class:** Marksman
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 4
- **Rarity:** S

## Kit

**Ultimate, Wild Sniper.** She dashes to a tile and fires a penetrating bolt down the line, damaging every enemy it passes through and **stunning them for 2s**.

**Skill I, Sweet Encounter.** A whip swing on a 12s cooldown that damages enemies, **knocks them back 2 tiles and immobilises them for 3s**. Enemies standing behind the target take collision damage at higher skill levels.

**Skill II, Scorching Grit.** An explosive bolt with area damage around the target. Higher levels widen the blast.

## Tier gates

**She stands at Epic, so three of her six pieces are dark.** That is the lowest a pulled S-Level hero can be. See [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).

| Piece | Gate | Live at Epic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wild Sniper | none | yes |
| Sweet Encounter | none | yes |
| Scorching Grit | none | yes |
| Hero Focus | Legendary+ | **no** |
| Sleight of Hand | Mythic+ | **no** |
| Enhance Force | Supreme+ | **no** |

**Hero Focus** grants +12 Haste for the fight, and +2 more after she damages 3 different enemies inside 3s.

**Sleight of Hand is her Exclusive Equipment and needs Mythic+.** It casts Sweet Encounter twice at battle start and adds stat theft on top, so the control half of her kit doubles at that tier and not before.

**Enhance Force needs Supreme+** and heals her for 10% of max HP whenever Wild Sniper connects.

## Best for

**Control that carries its own damage.** A stun, an immobilise, a knockback and two area hits sit in one Marksman slot, so she pays into the survival sum by stopping enemy casts and into the damage sum at the same time. See [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md) and [../team-archetypes.md](../team-archetypes.md).

**Range 4 is short for a Marksman**, so she stands nearer the front than Zanie at 20 or Hugin at 20, and the back row will not reach with Sweet Encounter. Place her in the middle. See [../formation.md](../formation.md).

**Her faction has no open progress left.** Tower of Light is cleared at 90/90, so the Lightbearer-locked reason to build a Lightbearer is spent. Her case has to be made in the modes that take any faction. See [../legend-trial.md](../legend-trial.md) and [../../account/progression.md](../../account/progression.md).
