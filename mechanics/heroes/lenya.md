# Lenya

> **Covers:** Lenya kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

- **Faction:** Wilder
- **Class:** Rogue
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 1
- **Level:** A

## Kit

Ultimate targets the enemy dealing the most cumulative damage, kicks them back, then starts a 12s duel where both prioritise each other. Tornado Strike blocks and retaliates for up to 130% with a stun. Flurry Kicks adds 15 Crit and follow-up kicks up to 240%. Winning Resolve adds 55 Crit DMG Boost and 15% ATK during a duel.

## Best for

PvP and boss. Isolates and kills a priority target.
