# Shadewing

> **Covers:** Shadewing kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-31

- **Faction:** Graveborn
- **Class:** Rogue
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 1
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

Crimson Venom deals rolling damage that scales with enemy HP already lost. Withering Curse cuts defences and sets off damage bursts. Razor Talons strikes twice then bleeds.

## Best for

Single-target damage through stacked curses, and it is **all damage out and no time alive**: nothing in the kit heals, shields, taunts or controls, and he fights at range 1. That is the axis the disagreements below run on.

**Campaign** `unverified`: rated **A+** [afk.global Campaign tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated], **A** as PvE [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated] and **B** in story mode [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated].

**Dream Realm** `unverified`: **A** [afk.global Dream Realm tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated], **B+** [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated] and **C** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. allclash gives its reason: his true damage suits a boss, but most of his kit is AoE and an AoE kit is wasted on one enemy.

**Dream Realm Endless, rated A** [afk.global Dream Realm Endless tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated]. His rolling damage reads HP already lost, which is the payoff Endless is built around.

**PvP** `unverified`: **A** [afk.global PvP tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated], **B+** [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated] and **B** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. allclash reads him as unable to survive long enough in PvP to land his stacks, which the kit supports: curses need time, and a range-1 body with no defensive line does not get it.

## Tier gate

**He is Elite, the lowest ascension tier in the game, and no list is describing that hero.** Hero Focus needs **Legendary+**, five steps up. Exclusive Equipment needs **Mythic+**, seven steps up. None of the three lists states an investment level, so every letter above is a ceiling.

He is **A-Level**, so the Dream Store stocks his copies and ascending him is a Dream Fragment purchase rather than a banner pull. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md), [../progression-systems.md](../progression-systems.md) and [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).
