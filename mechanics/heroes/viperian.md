# Viperian

> **Covers:** Viperian kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-31

- **Faction:** Graveborn
- **Class:** Mage
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 5
- **Level:** A

## Kit

Spiritual Viper is the Ultimate and the whole hero. He burns his own HP at battle start to send Darkvipers to possess **every** enemy, and holds them until his HP falls below 60%. Each Darkviper costs 7% of his max HP. Possessed enemies lose **1% HP per second** and drop **20 Energy** whenever they are hit. He recalls a Darkviper to recover 14% HP when he falls below 30%.

Tearing Bite fires from every Darkviper once every 3s for 65% damage to all possessed enemies. Its active form burns 10% of his max HP to deal 140% to one target and cut **60 Energy**. Vitality Extract drains the healthiest enemy and heals him for the damage dealt.

`unverified`: the Vitality Extract figure. afk.global gives 240%, the wiki gives 210%. Both are listed rather than averaged.

## Tier gates

**Two of his skills are locked on this account.** He is **Elite** in [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).

- **Hero Focus**, +12 Haste for the whole battle, needs **Legendary+**.
- **Crimson Waltz**, a 420% hit on all enemies bought with 30% of his HP, needs **Mythic+**.

So the guides describing a Haste-carrying AoE nuker are describing a hero four ascensions away. What is live is the possession Ultimate, Tearing Bite and Vitality Extract. See [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md).

## Best for

Campaign, rated B. Boss and PvP both rate C, because possession pays per enemy and a boss is one enemy.

**His damage is percentage-of-HP and his control is Energy denial.** The 1% per second and the Energy drain both ignore enemy defence, which is the damage type an uncapped ladder cannot resist. Against a spread of enemies that grows every second the fight runs. See [../team-archetypes.md](../team-archetypes.md).

**He pays his own HP for all of it**, so he needs a healer or mitigation beside him rather than a second damage dealer.
