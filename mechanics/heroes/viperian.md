# Viperian

> **Covers:** Viperian kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-31

- **Faction:** Graveborn
- **Class:** Mage
- **Damage:** Magic
- **Range:** 5
- **Rarity:** A

## Kit

Spiritual Viper is the Ultimate and the whole hero. He burns his own HP at battle start to send Darkvipers to possess **every** enemy, and holds them until his HP falls below 60%. Each Darkviper costs 7% of his max HP. Possessed enemies lose **1% HP per second** and drop **20 Energy** whenever they are hit. He recalls a Darkviper to recover 14% HP when he falls below 30%.

Tearing Bite fires from every Darkviper once every 3s for 65% damage to all possessed enemies. Its active form burns 10% of his max HP to deal 140% to one target and cut **60 Energy**. Vitality Extract drains the healthiest enemy and heals him for the damage dealt.

`unverified`: the Vitality Extract figure. afk.global gives 240%, the wiki gives 210%. Both are listed rather than averaged.

## Best for

**His damage is percentage-of-HP and his control is Energy denial.** The 1% per second and the Energy drain both ignore enemy defence, which is the damage type an uncapped ladder cannot resist. He wants a spread of enemies, because possession pays per enemy. See [../team-archetypes.md](../team-archetypes.md).

**He pays his own HP for all of it**, so he needs a healer or mitigation beside him rather than a second damage dealer.

**Campaign** `unverified`: rated **B** [afk.global Campaign tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated], **C+** as PvE [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated] and **C** in story mode [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. His best mode, because it is the one that reliably puts a crowd in front of him.

**Dream Realm** `unverified`: **C** [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated], **C** [afk.global Dream Realm tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated] and **D** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. A boss is one enemy, and possession pays per enemy.

**Dream Realm Endless, rated C** [afk.global Dream Realm Endless tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated], for the same reason.

**PvP** `unverified`: **B** [allclash, 2026-08-26, no investment stated], **C** [afk.global PvP tab, 2026-08-20, investment not stated] and **D** [Pocket Tactics, 2026-08-17, investment not stated]. The Energy cut is the case for the higher reading, since Arena is decided by who fires an Ultimate first.

allclash summarises him as never having reached relevance in any game mode, which is the harshest read any of the three gives a hero this account owns.

## Tier gate

**Two of his skills are locked on this account.** He is **Elite**, the lowest ascension tier.

- **Hero Focus**, +12 Haste for the whole battle, needs **Legendary+**, five steps up.
- **Crimson Waltz**, a 420% hit on all enemies bought with 30% of his HP, needs **Mythic+**, seven steps up.

So the guides describing a Haste-carrying AoE nuker are describing a hero seven ascensions away, and none of the three states an investment level. What is live is the possession Ultimate, Tearing Bite and Vitality Extract. See [../hero-scoring.md](../hero-scoring.md) and [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md).

He is **A-Level**, so the Dream Store stocks his copies. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md).
