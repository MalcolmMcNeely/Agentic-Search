# Valka

> **Covers:** Valka kit, stats and best use.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-31

- **Faction:** Graveborn
- **Class:** Warrior
- **Damage:** Physical
- **Range:** 1
- **Level:** A

## Kit

Ultimate Blooming Terror stacks panic through normal attacks then unleashes true-damage slashes scaled to enemy max HP. Spectral Bulwark shields nearby allies and raises their ATK SPD.

## Hero Focus

**Live.** It unlocks at **Legendary+**, which is exactly the tier she now holds in [../../account/roster.md](../../account/roster.md), so it switched on with the ascension.

**It raises her own ATK SPD by 18 during battle.** Two independent readings give the same 18, and allclash agrees the effect is attack speed and names level 3 the goal. `?` the level 2 and 3 figures; no source publishes them.

**ATK SPD is the right stat for her, because normal attacks are what feed her Ultimate.** Blooming Terror stacks panic through them and then converts the stacks into true damage scaled to the target's **max** HP. Faster normal attacks means more stacks, so the Hero Focus points at her one scaling loop rather than at a flat stat.

`?` which of the three levels this account holds. Hero Focus levels are bought with **Tidal Essence**, not earned by ascending, so the tier alone does not settle it.

## Tier gates

**Mythic+ is three tiers away**, so her Exclusive Equipment is far off. She is **A-Level**, so Legendary+ to Mythic costs **10 Soul Sigils** and Mythic to Mythic+ another **12**, and the **Dream Store does not stock her**, confirmed from the account, so every copy comes off a banner. See [../dream-store.md](../dream-store.md).

## Best for

Boss and PvP. Her true damage scales off the enemy's **max** HP, which is the stat a boss has most of, and it ignores defence outright. She holds the front in the **Skyclops** and **Tower of Eternity** comps and in a **Battle Drills** team. See [../../account/teams.md](../../account/teams.md).

## Notes

Weak in ordinary campaign content.
