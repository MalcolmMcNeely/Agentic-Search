# Honor Duel

> **Covers:** how an Honor Duel run works, the four starting combos, the two-stage deploy, the Duel Store, the mode's own hero pool and per-hero modifiers, and how to pick.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-31

Honor Duel is fair-play PvP. The account roster does not enter it. Every run hands you game-provided heroes and you build from there, so nothing in `account/` is relevant to a pick here.

## The run

- A run ends at **9 wins** or **3 losses**. You may forfeit early.
- Entry costs one Duel Letter. **5 free letters a day**, 5 held at most, up to **30 more per day** buyable with Gold.
- Rewards are Honor Points (seasonal leaderboard), Gems, Gold and Invite Letters.

## The starting combo — the four options

At the start of a run the game offers **four** starting combos. Each is one **Artifact + 3 heroes + Honor Badges**.

| Option | What it is | Badges |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Three named combos | Pre-made and visible before you commit. Named by faction (Mauler, Lightbearer, Wilder, Graveborn) or by role (Frontline). The three on offer are drawn fresh each run | 60 |
| Random | Random artifact and 3 random heroes, hidden until you commit | 70 |

Random pays **+10 badges** for the unknown. That is the whole trade.

The **artifact is the important half of the pick**, not the heroes. It shapes the run and the heroes get replaced from the shop. Artifacts with no faction or hero restriction keep the most options open.

## The deploy phase

Two stages per battle:

1. Both players place **3 heroes** at the same time. Both formations are then revealed.
2. Both players place their remaining **2 heroes**, now able to see the enemy.

Heroes cannot be moved after being deployed and revealed. The last two picks are the counter-pick, so hold flexible heroes back.

## The Duel Store

- Badges are earned during matches and spent in the Duel Store.
- The store levels up as the run goes: **Level 2 after 1 battle, Level 3 after 3, Level 4 after 5, Level 5 after 8**. Higher levels stock better items.
- Buys include single heroes, faction-specific heroes, and a **3-random-heroes pack for 18 badges**. Early on the random packs give more bodies per badge than named singles.
- Duplicates ascend a hero: **2 duplicates makes it Legendary, 4 more makes it the next tier up**. `unverified` — one source calls that tier Mystic, another calls it Mythic.
- Each hero wears **one** piece of Equipment. Gear can be swapped freely from the Formation menu.

## The mode re-tunes its own heroes

Two rules make a hero's open-roster reputation the wrong instrument in here, and both belong to the mode.

**The pool is limited, and it is patched.** Only **Lightbearer, Mauler, Wilder and Graveborn** heroes can be bought and built up. Celestial heroes appear as single-use trial copies, and no Hypogean or Dimensional hero is in the pool at all. Version 1.7.3 dropped Sonja, Evie, Nara and Hodgkin and added Valen, Peggy, Valka and Callan. Half of any roster ranking is therefore about heroes this mode never offers.

**A hero can carry a modifier that applies inside Honor Duel and nowhere else**, on damage dealt, damage taken and Haste, and each patch re-tunes them. At 1.7.3:

| Hero | Modifier, Honor Duel only |
| --- | --- |
| Silvina | +20% damage dealt, 10% less damage taken |
| Valen | +15% damage dealt, and less damage taken. `unverified`: the patch note garbles the second figure |
| Gwyneth | 15% less damage dealt, 10% more damage taken, ATK SPD -20 |
| Cyran | 15% less damage dealt, 10% more damage taken, Haste -20 |
| Bonnie and Nerion | modifier removed, both now fight at their normal numbers |

The size of that swing is the whole point. Cyran is an **S+ PvP** hero on the open roster [allclash 2026-08-26, investment not stated per hero], and he walks into Honor Duel with his damage cut, his damage taken raised and 20 Haste gone. Read the modifier before the reputation.

**No current source ranks anything for this mode.** Nothing published since **2024-04-04** ranks heroes, factions or starting combos for Honor Duel. That is past the 12-month limit, it predates the 1.7.3 re-tune aimed at this mode alone, and the gap is recorded in [tier-list-sources.md](tier-list-sources.md). There is no letter to borrow here, so rank what the run actually offers with [hero-scoring.md](hero-scoring.md), which needs no guide.

## How to build

- Target shape is **2 tanks, 1 main DPS, 1 support, 1 flex**.
- Depth loses to quality. A tight group of Legendary-or-better heroes beats a wide bench of un-ascended ones.
- **Score every hero the run offers on the two sums**, time alive and damage out. The offer is drawn fresh each run, so the ranking is made at the table, not carried in. See [hero-scoring.md](hero-scoring.md).
- **Concentrate the badges, and concentrate them on the main DPS.** Duplicates are the only ascension the mode has, so badges spread across five heroes buy five un-ascended bodies while the same badges on one cross a tier. An ascension multiplies the hero it lands on, which makes it worth most on the largest damage number on the team.
- **No hero here carries Exclusive Equipment.** The in-run ladder stops one tier above Legendary, and the EX weapon and its extra skill unlock at **Mythic+**. A hero whose guide reputation rests on that skill arrives without it, so score the three base skills only. See [progression-systems.md](progression-systems.md).
- **Get a healer early, and count control as part of the same job.** An enemy that cannot act deals no damage, which buys the same seconds a heal does and also stops burst.
- **Hold the answers back for the last two placements.** The counter-pick stage rewards control and mitigation, because both cancel a formation you can now see. A second damage dealer answers nothing that was revealed.
- **Faction is a stat bonus, not a ranking.** Three same-faction heroes pay +10% ATK and HP, four pay +18%, five pay +22%, and the counter wheel adds or removes 15% on top. All four pool factions sit on that wheel, so take the faction the artifact and the shop can actually deepen. See [factions.md](factions.md).

## Which of the four to take

- Take a **named combo** when its artifact carries no faction or hero restriction, or when its three heroes already share a faction the shop can deepen. Knowing the artifact up front is worth more than 10 badges.
- Take **Random** when all three named combos hang on a restricted artifact, or when the run is casual and the weekly rewards are already claimed.
