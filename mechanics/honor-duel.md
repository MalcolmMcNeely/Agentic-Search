# Honor Duel

> **Covers:** how an Honor Duel run works, the four starting combos, the two-stage deploy, the Duel Store, and how to pick.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

Honor Duel is fair-play PvP. The account roster does not enter it. Every run hands you game-provided heroes and you build from there, so nothing in `account/` is relevant to a pick here.

## The run

- A run ends at **9 wins** or **3 losses**. You may forfeit early.
- Entry costs one Duel Letter. **5 free letters a day**, 5 held at most, up to **30 more per day** buyable with Gold.
- Rewards are Honor Points (seasonal leaderboard), Gems, Gold and Invite Letters.

## The starting combo — the four options

At the start of a run the game offers **four** starting combos. Each is one **Artifact + 3 heroes + Honor Badges**.

| Option | What it is | Badges |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Three named combos | Pre-made and visible before you commit. Named by faction (Mauler, Lightbearer, Wilder, Graveborn) or by role (Frontline). The three on offer are drawn fresh each run | 60 |
| Random | Random artifact and 3 random heroes, hidden until you commit | 70 |

Random pays **+10 badges** for the unknown. That is the whole trade.

The **artifact is the important half of the pick**, not the heroes. It shapes the run and the heroes get replaced from the shop. Artifacts with no faction or hero restriction keep the most options open.

## The deploy phase

Two stages per battle:

1. Both players place **3 heroes** at the same time. Both formations are then revealed.
2. Both players place their remaining **2 heroes**, now able to see the enemy.

Heroes cannot be moved after being deployed and revealed. The last two picks are the counter-pick, so hold flexible heroes back.

## The Duel Store

- Badges are earned during matches and spent in the Duel Store.
- The store levels up as the run goes: **Level 2 after 1 battle, Level 3 after 3, Level 4 after 5, Level 5 after 8**. Higher levels stock better items.
- Buys include single heroes, faction-specific heroes, and a **3-random-heroes pack for 18 badges**. Early on the random packs give more bodies per badge than named singles.
- Duplicates ascend a hero: **2 duplicates makes it Legendary, 4 more makes it the next tier up**. `unverified` — one source calls that tier Mystic, another calls it Mythic.
- Each hero wears **one** piece of Equipment. Gear can be swapped freely from the Formation menu.

## How to build

- Target shape is **2 tanks, 1 main DPS, 1 support, 1 flex**. Funnel badges into the main DPS first.
- Depth loses to quality. A tight group of Legendary-or-better heroes beats a wide bench of un-ascended ones.
- Get a healer early. Tank investment is rated the highest-value upgrade.
- Guides rate **Graveborn** the strongest faction core in this mode (Cecia, Thoran, Igor, with Hewynn healing). **Lightbearer** is next, on Mirael as the nuker with Lucius and Temesia in front. **Mauler** is rated weakest as a start and wants Bone Scroll to work.

## Which of the four to take

- Take a **named combo** when one of the three is Graveborn or Lightbearer, or when you want a known artifact to plan around. Knowing the artifact up front is worth more than 10 badges.
- Take **Random** when the three named combos on offer are all weak, or when the run is casual and the weekly rewards are already claimed.
