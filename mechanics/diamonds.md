# Diamonds

> **Covers:** every diamond sink in the game, its price and cap, the spend order, and what never to buy.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-30

Diamonds are the one currency that is both scarce and spendable everywhere, so most of them get wasted. Two facts set the whole plan:

- **The cheapest pull is not on a banner.** The Guild Store sells one discounted Invite Letter a day for **210 diamonds**, down from 300. A ten-pull costs 2,700, or 270 a pull. Buying the daily letter is 30% off a single pull and 22% off a ten-pull, every day, forever.
- **The Emporium holds two shops diamonds cannot reach.** The Dream Store takes Dream Fragments only, earned in Dream Realm, and it is the cheapest source of A-Level hero copies. See [dream-store.md](dream-store.md). The Guild Store's other shelf takes Guild Medals and sells Celestial and Hypogean heroes outright, one sigil per hero unlocked. See [guild-store.md](guild-store.md).
- **Diamonds reach Stargaze after all.** The Stargaze banner itself takes Stellar Crystals only, but the Guild Store sells crystals for **400 diamonds**, capped at **6 a month**. See [recruitment.md](recruitment.md).

## Every diamond sink

| Sink | Price | Cap | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Guild Store, discounted Invite Letter | 210 diamonds | 1 a day | **buy every day.** The best diamond rate in the game |
| Guild Store, Stellar Crystal | 400 diamonds | 6 a month | **buy the cap**, once Stargaze is unlocked and a target is set |
| All-Hero Recruitment, ten-pull | 2,700 diamonds | none | the default home for leftover diamonds |
| Rate Up Recruitment, ten-pull | 2,700 diamonds | none | only when the featured hero is wanted. 40 pity, so 10,800 guarantees them |
| All-Hero or Rate Up, single pull | 300 diamonds | none | **never.** Always pull in tens |
| Arena challenge tickets | `unverified`, rises with each ticket bought | 5 free a day, 5 more buyable | only when a rank-up is within reach |
| Quick AFK, instant AFK rewards | `unverified` | 2 a day | only on an account far enough in that the drops are rare materials |
| Exp, Gold, standard Equipment | any | any | **never.** All three arrive free from the AFK rewards and the story |

`unverified` on the Arena ticket price: sources agree the cost climbs with each extra ticket in a day and none names the figures. `unverified` on Quick AFK: the twice-a-day cap is sourced, the diamond cost is not.

Epic Invite Letters also appear in the Guild Store's monthly stock, but the currency is `unverified`. afk.guide reads the monthly shelf as Guild Coins; prydwen and gameleap say to buy the letters without naming what pays for them. Check the price tag in game before planning around it.

The Recruitment Store sells Stellar Crystals too, at 310 Dolly Tickets discounted, 1 a month, or 620 each for 3 a month. Dolly Tickets come from pulling, not from diamonds, so that shelf is free money rather than a diamond decision.

## The spend order

1. **The daily 210-diamond Invite Letter.** 6,300 a month for 30 pulls' worth of value at a 30% discount. Nothing else comes close.
2. **The 6 monthly Stellar Crystals**, 2,400 diamonds, once a Stargaze target is picked. Stargaze pity is fixed at 40 and crystals trickle in, so this is a standing order, not a purchase.
3. **Bank the rest.** Guides put the target for a wanted rate-up banner at roughly **30,000 diamonds**, enough to cover the 40-pull pity twice over with room for the next one.
4. **All-Hero ten-pulls** with what is left over, for faction depth and duplicate copies.

A month of steps 1 and 2 costs about **8,700 diamonds**. An account that cannot cover that is not saving for a rate-up, it is choosing between the daily letter and the bank. Take the daily letter.

## Against this account

[../account/progression.md](../account/progression.md) holds **17,000 diamonds**, enough for roughly two months of the daily letter and the crystal cap together.

**Do not pull the current rate-up.** Cyran and Thador are both B+ tier and both already owned. See [recruitment.md](recruitment.md).

**Stargaze is marked unlocked, the target is Elijah & Lailah and pity is 0.** Crystals do not appear in either shop until Stargazing unlocks at 400 total pulls, so the 400-diamond shelf is only real if that unlock is genuine. Check the shelf, then buy the cap. The Guild Medal shelf unlocks the same hero outright for 30,000 Guild Medals, so spend there first. See [guild-store.md](guild-store.md).

**Arena tickets and Quick AFK are both wrong for this account.** Arena progress is unrecorded, and Quick AFK pays off on accounts deep enough for rare drops.
