# Ravaged Realm

> **Covers:** the Ravaged Realm event, the squad lock that decides its comps, how it scores, what the Valor Store sells, and the Giant Golem boss it currently fields.
> **Updated:** 2026-09-04

A recurring event in which one boss is fought over about two and a half weeks for a district ranking. It runs roughly every four months and has run **seven** times: Setsahara, Thalassa, Novik, Azkarion'Sol, Azora, Gervan, and the **Giant Golem** rotation live now.

**The squad lock is the whole mode.** Every other Dream-Realm-shaped boss race lets the account field its five best heroes. This one does not, and that is what any comp for it turns on.

## Three phases

| Phase | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Squad Rotations** | one squad is active each day. Only that squad's heroes may be fielded. Rankings reset daily |
| **Ultimate Siege** | every squad is playable at once. Rankings stop resetting, and this is the ranking that pays |
| **Valor Awards** | the reward phase. The Valor Store closes at the end of it |

Daily ranking rewards land at 02:00 UTC+2 during Squad Rotations. The daily reset is at 16:00 UTC-8.

`unverified`: the two reset times above are stated on the mode page in two different time zones and never reconciled, and the Gervan rotation page states a third figure, 00:00 UTC, for the same daily payout. Read the timer in game.

## How it scores

Six difficulties: Common, Hard, Epic, Hell I, Hell II, Hell III, then **Endless Mode**, which opens once all six are cleared for that boss. In Endless the boss cannot be killed and the score is damage dealt.

**Each squad's result is tallied separately**, so a weak squad day is not carried by a strong one.

- **Squad Rotations.** Ranked daily on the total damage that day's squad dealt and the highest difficulty it cleared.
- **Ultimate Siege.** Ranked on the highest difficulty any squad completed, then on damage. **100% of the damage on the highest difficulty cleared counts, plus 20% of the damage on the second-highest.**

A server already in a district is ranked against the district. Otherwise it is ranked against the server.

## What it pays

Difficulty clear rewards pay **Grimstones** at 10%, 30%, 50% and 70% of the boss HP bar and **Diamonds** at 100%. Endless pays Diamonds at 2,500M, 7,500M, 12B, 17B and 25B damage.

**The Valor Store is the reason to care.** Grimstones buy **Temporal Essence: five lots of 5, for 1,600 to 2,800 Grimstones each, 25 in total.** Temporal Essence cannot be bought anywhere else on this account and is its tightest currency, with four Exclusive Equipment upgrades queued behind it. The store also stocks Stellar Crystals, Tidal Essence, A-Level Soulstones and Omni-Acorns. Unspent Grimstones convert to Gold at 1:300 when the store closes, which is close to nothing. **Spend them all.** See [../account/equipment.md](../account/equipment.md) and [equipment-and-charms.md](equipment-and-charms.md).

Ranking rewards pay Twilight Essence, Branco Essence, Ultra Radiant Crests, Stellar Crystals, avatar frames and, in the top 100, cosmetics.

## The Giant Golem

The boss fielded now. Warrior, **Other** faction, **Physical** damage, base range **20**.

**Other sits outside the counter wheel**, so no owned hero gains or loses the ±15%. Faction choice here buys the team stat bonus and nothing else. See [factions.md](factions.md).

**Range 20 reaches the whole grid.** No row is safe, and nothing in the kit targets a tile, a cluster or the nearest hero. So **bunching the team costs nothing in this fight**, and formation is free to serve buff geometry instead of safety. That is the opposite of King Croaker, whose Water Bomb aims at the densest area. See [formation.md](formation.md) and [king-croaker.md](king-croaker.md).

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Shattered Comet** | charges, then deals **80% ATK six times to all enemies**. **The Golem is paralysed for a while after casting it** |
| **Astral Strike** | smashes the ground for **350% ATK to all enemies**. Anyone who takes the damage is **knocked down for 6s** and loses **50 Haste for 15s**, which does not stack. **A shield blocks all of it** |
| **Ultra Sweep** | sweeps for **200% ATK to all enemies** and makes them **lose 5% of their max HP over 6s** |
| **Energy Realm** | passive. **Doubles the duration of every shield on the field**, on both sides |

`unverified`: **the four percentages come from one source**, the Fandom skill table, and describe the Golem's Battle Drills and Starter Story build. The Ravaged Realm version is scaled up: Gervan was fielded at level 1228. No source publishes the event build's numbers. The shapes hold; the figures are a guide.

### What the kit demands

**Bring shields, not heals.** Two of the four skills say so and they compound.

1. **Astral Strike's knockdown and Haste cut are blocked by shields**, and nothing else in the game stops them. Six seconds of knockdown across the whole team, over and over, is the largest damage loss on offer here, and Haste is the stat that sets how often everyone casts.
2. **Energy Realm doubles every shield's duration**, so the boss pays for the counter to its own crowd control. A shield with 8s of uptime holds for 16.

**Ultra Sweep is percentage max-HP loss**, so a big HP pool does not blunt it and a flat heal races it rather than beating it. A shield eats it before it lands.

**Shattered Comet's paralysis is a free damage window.** The Golem stops after casting it, so a comp that banks Energy and dumps Ultimates into that gap scores more than one that fires on cooldown.

**Every skill is AoE-to-all, so there is no aggro to manage and no carry to hide.** A tank buys the team nothing by soaking, and only earns its slot by what it hands out.
