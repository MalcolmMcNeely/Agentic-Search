# Snow Stomper

> **Covers:** the Snow Stomper Dream Realm boss, its skills, and the team shape that beats it.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-29

One of the four Dream Realm bosses. An ice-themed Elite Boss. Dream Realm is a damage race against a timer, so the score is damage dealt, not a kill. See [game-modes.md](game-modes.md).

## Faction

**Not published.** No source found states Snow Stomper's faction. Treat the counter wheel in [factions.md](factions.md) as unusable here and pick heroes on kit and gear instead.

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Opener | Hits the hero closest to it, melee or ranged, then follows with an AoE Frost attack |
| Frost | Three waves of damage to every hero standing in front of it, in a radius |
| Tearing Bite | Single target, damage equal to 5% of max HP |
| Snowball | Hits your **healthiest** hero and knocks them down for 1s |
| Blizzard | Cast 10s into the fight, lasts 20s, repeats every 30s, three times per fight |
| Yeti Cell (Ultimate) | Turns one hero into a snowman. They cannot attack and take continuous damage |

`unverified`, one source each, both readings kept:

- gfinityesports says Blizzard **silences** all heroes for its 20s. Other guides describe it only as a blizzard with no silence.
- playafkjourney says Yeti Cell targets the **highest-damage** hero. dotesports says it takes any one hero.

Break the Yeti Cell snowman with **normal attacks**, so a hero with strong normal attacks earns its slot.

## Team shape

Four roles, agreed across guides:

1. **DPS carry** — high single-target damage. Self-sustain or life drain preferred.
2. **Debuffer** — strips the boss's defence. This is the role the guides weight highest.
3. **Buffer** — raises team damage.
4. **Healer** — optional but keeps the damage flowing to the timer.

**Prefer ranged heroes.** `unverified`, one source. The reasoning given is that the frontal Frost cone hits up to three heroes at once, so melee and short-range heroes lose uptime. Taken at face value it argues for a backline-heavy comp with one body up front to absorb the opener.

## Artifact

**Starshard Spell**, named by several guides for this boss. It fires a true-damage wave every 4 Ultimates, and true damage ignores the boss's defence.
