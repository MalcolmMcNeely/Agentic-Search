# Legend Trial

> **Covers:** the four Legend Trial towers, which faction each locks to, the weekday rotation, floor count, and what the faction lock does to team building.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-30

Legend Trial is the old Factional Tower. It unlocks at AFK Stage 166. One own team per floor, and the team is faction-locked.

## The four towers

| Tower | Faction | Open on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tower of Light | Lightbearer | Monday, Friday, Sunday |
| Tower of Will | Mauler | Tuesday, Friday, Sunday |
| Tower of Nature | Wilder | Wednesday, Saturday, Sunday |
| Tower of Eternity | Graveborn | Thursday, Saturday, Sunday |

Two sources agree on all three columns. Each tower holds **90 floors**, 360 across all four.

Only the four standard factions get a tower. Celestial, Hypogean and Dimensional have none. See [factions.md](factions.md).

## The faction lock

Inside a tower you may deploy **only heroes of that tower's faction**. Every source states this the same way.

**Celestial and Hypogean heroes are locked out too.** They wildcard into any faction everywhere else in the game, but not here. **Confirmed from the account:** Aurora, the account's only Celestial, cannot be selected in Tower of Nature. No guide states this outright; they only imply it by calling the towers "the non-Celestial and non-Hypogean Factions". The account settles it.

Dimensional heroes do not wildcard, so they are out of every tower. Confirmed from the account, see [factions.md](factions.md).

## What this changes about team building

- **Faction depth beats a good mixed comp.** A tower needs five heroes of one faction at usable tiers. A faction with only three or four owned heroes cannot field a full team.
- **The faction stat bonus is always +22%.** Five same-faction heroes is forced, so the bonus is not a choice to weigh. See [factions.md](factions.md).
- **The counter wheel is not a choice either.** Your faction is fixed by the tower, so plan around taking -15% on the floors that counter you rather than around picking an advantage.
- **Awakening Spell is called out as strong here**, on a healer team or a hyper-carry. See [artifacts.md](artifacts.md).
- Rewards are Faction Acorns each floor, plus Invite Letters and Hero Essence every five floors.
