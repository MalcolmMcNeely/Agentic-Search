# King Croaker

> **Covers:** the King Croaker Dream Realm boss, its instant-kill bubble, its energy barrier, and the team shape that beats it.
> **Updated:** 2026-08-31

One of the Dream Realm bosses, and one of the eight in the Season 7 rotation. A giant frog. Dream Realm is a damage race against a timer, so the score is damage dealt, not a kill. See [game-modes.md](game-modes.md). The other bosses written up are [snow-stomper.md](snow-stomper.md) and [skyclops.md](skyclops.md).

**King Croaker is the boss that deletes whichever hero stands closest to it.** Everything below follows from that.

## Faction

A **Mage of the "Other" faction at base range 20**, so it reaches any tile on the grid. Same reading as [skyclops.md](skyclops.md), and the counter wheel in [factions.md](factions.md) has no entry for it. Pick heroes on kit and survivability, not on faction advantage.

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Magic Bubble | Releases a bubble at the **nearest** hero, which kills it instantly. Fires three times a fight |
| Magic Barrier (Ultimate) | Regains Energy every second and raises a barrier when Energy fills. The barrier cuts incoming damage. Taking damage drains the Energy |
| Water Bomb | Charges, then drops a bomb on the area holding the **most** heroes, damaging everything within 2 tiles |
| Tail Smash | Damages heroes within 1 tile, knocks them back a tile and stuns them |

`unverified`: **every number in this fight.** The percentages, the timings and the barrier strength are stated only by sources with no findable date, or by sources older than 12 months. Nothing published in the last year confirms them. The readings found were 220% for Water Bomb, 240% for Tail Smash, a 2.5s stun, bubbles at the 55s, 35s and 15s marks, 60 Energy a second, and a barrier blocking up to 90% at boss levels 5 and 6. Treat the shape of each skill as sound and the figures as untrusted.

`unverified`: **the fight length.** Two undated sources give 90 seconds and 30 to 34 boss actions. No in-window source states it.

**The bubble picks by distance, not by role.** It is not aimed at the tank, it is aimed at whoever stands nearest. That makes the front tile a slot the comp chooses to fill rather than one it fills by habit.

## Team shape

1. **A body that survives the bubble, or does not mind dying to it.** This is the whole fight. The guides all name Thoran, whose revive lets him eat it twice. **This account does not own Thoran.** See [../account/roster.md](../account/roster.md).
2. **Two or three carries, not one.** Every source agrees no single carry puts up enough damage here.
3. **A healer that heals continuously,** because the AoE keeps landing on the back row.
4. **A debuffer,** to strip defence and speed the barrier down.
5. **Keep everything else ranged.** A short-range hero that walks forward can steal the bubble from the hero picked to eat it.

**Do not bunch the team.** Water Bomb aims at the area holding the most heroes, so a tight formation feeds it. This costs the account something real: Yamato & Gabumon's EX weapon fires more missiles the closer allies stand to him. See [heroes/yamato-gabumon.md](heroes/yamato-gabumon.md).

## Artifact

**Starshard Spell**, named by every guide that names one. Its wave is true damage, which ignores the boss's defence.

`unverified`: **whether the Magic Barrier stops true damage.** The barrier is described as reducing damage, not as a defence stat, and no source says which. Starshard stays the pick because it is the guide choice and the account holds it at +8, but a run that scores poorly is a reason to test Blazing Spell +10 instead, whose DEF Penetration discounts every attack rather than one wave. See [artifacts.md](artifacts.md).

## Heroes the guides name

**The one in-window source is allclash, dated 25 August 2026**, and its meta team is **Phraesto, Rolan, Elijah & Lailah, Galahad and Agumon**. This account owns all five. That is the only Dream Realm guide lineup on this repo the account can field whole.

It still is not copied straight across. **Galahad is Legendary here and her Time Recast needs Mythic+**, so the hero that guide describes is not the hero this account fields. See [heroes/galahad.md](heroes/galahad.md) and the tier-gate rule in [hero-scoring.md](hero-scoring.md).

The older guides name Thoran, Smokey & Meerky, Kruger, Reinier, Marilee, Odie, Cecia, Hewynn and Rowan. Of those the account owns **Smokey & Meerky, Kruger, Cecia, Hewynn and Rowan**.

**allclash builds its teams on Magic Charms and Season artifacts, and this account has neither.** It is permanent-track only, and Magic Charms sit behind Season AFK Stage 305. So its artifact picks, Sorcery and Swift Heal, are unreachable. See [../account/progression.md](../account/progression.md) and [seasons.md](seasons.md).
