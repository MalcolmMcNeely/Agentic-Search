**Reading a partial update from the user.** The user states slot values in **slot order, left to right, and stops at the last slot that changed**. A slot they do not name did not change and carries over. Apply this to every gear dump.

### How the class Level is derived

The class Level is the **mean of the six slots, rounded down**.

No guide states this. It is settled from the account, on two readings that each rule out rounding to nearest:

| Class | Mean | Screen | Floor gives | Nearest would give |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tank | 197.50 | 197 | 197 | 198 |
| Warrior | 205.83 | 205 | 205 | 206 |

Tank is the decisive case. A mean of exactly 197.50 must round **up** to 198 under nearest, and the screen shows 197.

### How this account upgrades gear

**The user does not pick the slots.** They press **Quick Equip whenever the button is highlighted** and take what it gives.

So the upgrade priority table in [../mechanics/equipment-and-charms.md](../mechanics/equipment-and-charms.md) is not an action for this account. Most fed slots do not match it, and that is the game's choice, not the user ignoring advice. Do not hand that table over unless the user says they have started upgrading by hand.

**Quick Equip does not feed the lowest slot first.** Three classes carry a slot the button walks past while lifting every other slot around it: **Warrior Ring at 145**, against 200 to 230 elsewhere in that class; **Support Armor at 160**, against 195 to 220; **Rogue Boots at 160**, against 190 to 230. A lowest-slot-first rule cannot strand a slot 85 levels behind its siblings.

**Gap: what Quick Equip does optimise is unknown.** Searches across prydwen, the Fandom wiki, playafkjourney, gamerant and mejoress return pages on the equipment and forging systems but nothing on the button itself. Whether Quick Equip costs this account anything against hand-picking is open. Do not guess either way.

### Reading the spread

**Marksman leads at 209 and Tank is lowest at 197.** The spread is 12 levels across all six classes, so class gear no longer picks or drops a comp. Treat every class as equally geared when comparing two builds.

**Tank being lowest is a small cost to both `running` AFK comps.** Temesia fronts one and Daimon fronts the other, and both draw Tank at 197. Warrior sits 8 levels above at 205, so Daimon being a Tank rather than a Warrior is a slight gear loss. It is 8 levels on one hero, so it changes nothing.

**Warrior Ring at 145 is the one real hole.** It sits 85 below the other five Warrior slots and holds the class mean down on its own. Lift it to 230, where Helmet and Boots already sit, and Warrior reaches **220** and tops the table. It is the single highest-value slot on the account.

Every class sits **67 to 79 levels above** the Resonance Level of 130, and four slots reach 230, **100 above** it. Several guides claim gear cannot exceed the Resonance Level. This account disproves that. See the note in [../mechanics/equipment-and-charms.md](../mechanics/equipment-and-charms.md).

No slot sits below the Resonance Level. The lowest single slot is Warrior Ring at 145.
