**Reading a partial update from the user.** The user states slot values in **slot order, left to right, and stops at the last slot that changed**. A slot they do not name did not change and carries over. Apply this to every gear dump.

### How the class Level is derived

The class Level is the **mean of the six slots, rounded down**.

No guide states this. It is settled from the account, on two readings that each rule out rounding to nearest:

| Class | Mean | Screen | Floor gives | Nearest would give |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rogue | 206.67 | 206 | 206 | 207 |
| Warrior | 205.83 | 205 | 205 | 206 |

Both means sit past the halfway mark, so rounding to nearest would lift each one a level. The screen shows the lower number in both cases.

### How this account upgrades gear

**The user does not pick the slots.** They press **Quick Equip whenever the button is highlighted** and take what it gives.

So the upgrade priority table in [../mechanics/equipment-and-charms.md](../mechanics/equipment-and-charms.md) is not an action for this account. Most fed slots do not match it, and that is the game's choice, not the user ignoring advice. Do not hand that table over unless the user says they have started upgrading by hand.

**Quick Equip does not feed the lowest slot first.** Three picks prove it. It took Rogue Helmet from 230, the **joint-highest** slot in that class, while Rogue Boots sat at 160. It took Tank Weapon from 190 while Tank Gloves sat lower at 185. It also took Support Armor from 160, the lowest slot in its class, so it does not feed the highest slot first either. Two slots stay stranded far behind their siblings: **Warrior Ring at 145**, against 200 to 230 elsewhere in that class, and **Rogue Boots at 160**, against 190 to 240. A lowest-slot-first rule cannot leave a slot 85 levels behind.

**Gap: what Quick Equip does optimise is unknown.** Searches across prydwen, the Fandom wiki, playafkjourney, gamerant and mejoress return pages on the equipment and forging systems but nothing on the button itself. Whether Quick Equip costs this account anything against hand-picking is open. Do not guess either way.

### Reading the spread

**Support leads at 213 and Tank is lowest at 202.** The spread is 11 levels across all six classes, so class gear still does not pick or drop a comp on its own. Treat every class as near-equally geared when comparing two builds.

**Tank being lowest is a small cost to both `running` AFK comps.** Temesia fronts one and Daimon fronts the other, and both draw Tank at 202. Warrior sits 3 levels above at 205, so Daimon being a Tank rather than a Warrior is a slight gear loss. It is 3 levels on one hero, so it changes nothing.

**Warrior Ring at 145 is the one real hole.** It sits 85 below the other five Warrior slots and holds the class mean down on its own. Lift it to 230, where Helmet and Boots already sit, and Warrior reaches **220** and tops the table. It is the single highest-value slot on the account.

**Warrior Ring is also the only slot below the Resonance Level.** At 145 it now sits 5 under the Resonance Level of 150. Every other slot on the account is above it.

Every class sits **52 to 63 levels above** the Resonance Level of 150, and two slots reach the 240 cap, **90 above** it. Several guides claim gear cannot exceed the Resonance Level. This account disproves that. See the note in [../mechanics/equipment-and-charms.md](../mechanics/equipment-and-charms.md).
